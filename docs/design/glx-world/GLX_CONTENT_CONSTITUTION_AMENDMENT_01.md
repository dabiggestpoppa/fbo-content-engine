# GLX CONTENT CONSTITUTION — AMENDMENT 01
## Premium IP, Canon Authority & Production Separation

**Status:** Canonical amendment  
**Applies to:** GLX CONTENT CONSTITUTION and all GLX WORLD production

---

## 1. Purpose

This amendment clarifies the relationship between GLX WORLD and the broader FBO/MAD production infrastructure after review of the current content-farm stack.

The existing Content Constitution remains active.

This amendment has authority where it adds specificity or where older language could be interpreted as favoring volume over canon quality.

---

## 2. GLX WORLD Is Intellectual Property, Not a Farm Niche

GLX WORLD must not be treated as merely another `finance` niche or generic content template.

It is a first-class creative universe with:
- a protagonist
- canon
- locations
- visual languages
- story laws
- recurring entities
- artifacts
- transformations
- mysteries
- educational mythology
- long-term character progression

Generic finance templates may provide infrastructure patterns.

They do not define GLX voice or creative identity.

---

## 3. Creative Authority Hierarchy

For GLX WORLD, authority is:

1. Canonical world/character documents
2. GLX Content Constitution
3. GLX Production Constitution
4. Current season/series bible
5. Approved episode premise
6. Platform adaptation rules
7. Production automation
8. trend/template defaults

Lower levels may not silently override higher levels.

---

## 4. Quality Before Scale

The broad farm may optimize for systematic output.

GLX WORLD optimizes for durable brand equity.

Therefore:

> **Canon consistency > originality > story > substance > visual quality > efficiency > volume**

Scale is desirable only after quality survives it.

---

## 5. The Content Factory Must Become Invisible

The audience should never feel that GLX is mass-produced.

Recurring characters, templates, locations, and motifs are allowed because they create a coherent universe.

Mechanical repetition, generic hooks, interchangeable finance scripts, and visible automation sameness are not.

---

## 6. GLX Owns Its Voice

GLX must not inherit generic “authoritative finance” copy by default.

The canonical GLX voice is:
- exploratory
- evidence-aware
- conversational
- technically capable
- funny
- human
- willing to admit uncertainty

CEREBUS, GLX, and Chibi maintain their established Logic/Judgment/Emotion voice triangle.

---

## 7. GLX Owns Its Visual Grammar

The approved primary visual languages remain:
- Caricature — Reality
- Anime — Mythology/Action
- Chibi — Emotion
- Cinematic Glitch — Legend/Revelation

Each form exists for a reason.

The production system should select styles from narrative purpose rather than random variety.

---

## 8. Real Research Remains Real

GLX mythology may dramatize:
- liquidity
- volatility
- risk
- market structure
- macro flows
- trading psychology
- CEREBUS research

But dramatization does not permit fabrication of evidence.

Claims presented as tested, validated, measured, or historical must come from real sources or real research.

---

## 9. Worldbuilding Is Now the Primary Creative Workstream

With production infrastructure substantially present, the current priority is:

> **build the universe deep enough that production can draw from canon rather than inventing identity on demand.**

Immediate work proceeds through:
- Block 2 — The World
- Block 3 — Forces & Entities
- Block 4 — Market Mythology
- Block 5 — Narrative System
- Block 6 — Visual Canon
- Block 7 — Series Bible
- Block 8 — Expanded Voice Universe
- Block 9 — Audience Experience
- Block 10 — Seasons & Long-Term Lore
- Block 11 — Editorial Architecture

---

## 10. Final Clarification

GLX WORLD should never become:

> a content farm with a pink-hatted mascot.

It should become:

> **a living financial story universe whose production infrastructure happens to be exceptionally scalable.**
