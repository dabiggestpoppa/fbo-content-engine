# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 1A: EXACT VISUAL MODEL SHEET RULES

## Status
Canonical draft for GLX visual identity and cross-style consistency.

## Purpose
Block 1A locks the visual DNA of GLX so caricature, anime, chibi, cinematic, stickers, shorts, and future model sheets all depict the same recognizable protagonist.

# A.1 — CORE RECOGNITION PRIORITY
Preserve recognition in this order:
1. Pink fuzzy hat
2. Black sunglasses
3. Face + facial hair
4. Gold chain
5. Black GLX clothing
6. Posture / silhouette
7. Glitch accents

Simplify from the bottom upward. The hat and shades are the primary identity lock.

# A.2 — FACE CANON
GLX should have a medium-broad youthful face, strong but not superhero jawline, clean cheek structure, fuller lips, strong brow presence, visible mustache, and short beard/goatee. Never over-age or turn him into a generic action hero.

# A.3 — FACIAL HAIR
Baseline: mustache + short connected or near-connected goatee/beard, clean edges, not thick or long. Caricature may show texture; anime simplifies; chibi uses small markings; cinematic uses sharper realistic grooming.

# A.4 — SUNGLASSES
Black frames, dark lenses, slightly angular/rectangular, contemporary, medium-large, close to face. Not circular fashion glasses or default aviators.

Lens language:
- Normal — dark black
- Quant — chart/screen reflections
- Macro — maps/world grids
- Glitched — RGB leakage / eyes behind lenses
- Broken — cracked/displaced lens
- Ascendant — clean, minimal reflection

# A.5 — EYE REVEAL LAW
Eyes are normally hidden. Reveal only for shock, exhaustion, vulnerability, rage, serious fear, major realization, Glitch activation, injury, intimate moments, or transformation. Never remove the shades casually.

# A.6 — PINK HAT CANON
The single strongest physical brand element: oversized, fuzzy, bucket-like, plush, hot-pink/magenta, thickly textured, large-brimmed, visually soft and slightly exaggerated.

> **streetwear + cartoon prop + crown**

without literally becoming a crown.

# A.7 — HAT GEOMETRY
- Caricature: ~1.15–1.30× head width, thick/floppy brim, visible fuzz
- Anime: ~1.10–1.20×, cleaner/sharper silhouette
- Chibi: ~1.25–1.45×, deliberately huge
- Cinematic: closer to realistic but still oversized, high-detail texture

# A.8 — HAT BEHAVIOR
Confused = tilt. Angry = pulled lower. Exhausted = slipping. Running = bouncing. Explosion = briefly airborne. Victory = brim upward. Broken = crooked. Glitched = fragmenting/pixelating. Chibi panic = vibrating.

# A.9 — BODY CANON
Average-to-athletic, youthful, lean-medium frame, relaxed shoulders, natural proportions. Normal GLX is stylish rather than intimidating. Warrior may exaggerate anatomy.

# A.10 — PROPORTIONS BY STYLE
## Caricature
Slightly enlarged head/hands, normal-medium torso, expressive pose. Editorial illustration, not bobblehead.

## Anime
Longer limbs, dynamic torso, expressive hands, sharper posture, recognizable simplified face. Never generic anime protagonist in pink.

## Chibi
Head ~45–60% of body height, tiny torso/limbs, huge hat, extreme expressions. Emotion first.

## Cinematic
Realistic anatomy, slightly heroic proportions, detailed clothing/hat, strong silhouette. Movie-poster realism without identity drift.

# A.11 — SKIN TONE
Medium-dark warm brown skin, consistent across styles. Lighting may tint it but underlying identity stays stable. Avoid accidental lightening.

# A.12 — HAIR
Short, dark, natural texture, well groomed when visible. Hair should not dominate because the hat is primary. Major hairstyle changes require story reason.

# A.13 — WARDROBE SYSTEM
- GLX Core — black T-shirt, chain, black pants, hat, shades
- GLX Hoodie — oversized black hoodie, subtle pink accents
- Lab Fit — black technical overshirt, dark cargos, utility details
- Clean Casual — black jacket, dark shirt, chain
- Mentor — clean black jacket/overshirt, restrained branding
- Cinematic — structured black coat/jacket, subtle glitch trim, gold hardware

# A.14 — CLOTHING RULES
Black dominates. Pink appears mainly in hat/trim/lining. Gold in chain/hardware/symbols. Avoid full bright-pink outfits unless intentional comedy/event. The hat owns pink.

# A.15 — FOOTWEAR
Black sneakers, boots, or dark streetwear shoes. Warrior may become fantasy-tech. Chibi simplifies. Avoid random bright footwear.

# A.16 — JEWELRY
Gold chain is canonical. Bracelet/watch/rings optional. Gold signals identity, value and ambition—not excess.

# A.17 — LOGO USAGE
GLX is not a billboard. One dominant brand mark per outfit is usually enough. Character first, universe second, logos third.

# A.18 — SILHOUETTE LAW
Recognizable blacked-out silhouette: wide fuzzy hat, shades profile, chain curve, relaxed shoulders, loose dark streetwear, expressive hand posture. Chibi silhouette = giant hat + tiny body.

# A.19 — POSTURE
Rarely stiff. Shifted weight, slight lean, relaxed shoulders, one hand interacting, head angled. Natural confidence, never stock-photo posing.

# A.20 — HAND LANGUAGE
Common gestures: point at chart, stylus/notebook, adjust glasses, chin touch, grip weapon, keyboard, mug, palm-up “what the hell?” gesture. Hands are personality channels.

# A.21 — DEFAULT FACIAL STATES
- Neutral — slight closed-mouth smirk
- Analytical — straight mouth, chin tucked
- Confident — small grin
- Suspicious — tilt + raised brow
- Shocked — slightly open mouth, possible eye reveal
- Angry — tightened jaw
- Exhausted — slack mouth + crooked hat
- Amused — side grin
- Locked-In — minimal expression, focused posture

# A.22 — ART STYLE TRANSLATION LAW
> **Identity > style accuracy.**

If anime, chibi, realism or cinematic styling erases GLX recognition, the styling is wrong. The character must survive the style.

# A.23 — CARICATURE STYLE
Recognizable likeness, slightly enlarged head, detailed environment, expressive anatomy, clean linework, saturated color, editorial storytelling, humorous exaggeration, layered props. Best for research, behind-the-scenes, psychology, education and personal content.

# A.24 — ANIME STYLE
Confident lines, saturated color, cel shading, dynamic angles, warped perspective, action poses, energy trails. Best for battles, macro, story arcs and transformations.

# A.25 — CHIBI STYLE
Huge head and hat, tiny body, simplified face, exaggerated expression, sticker-friendly outline, minimal background. Best for reactions, memes, Discord, stickers and inner thoughts.

# A.26 — CINEMATIC STYLE
Detailed lighting, neon/glitch atmosphere, dramatic scale, environmental depth, realistic textures and poster composition. Best for launches, breakthroughs, lore reveals and high-stakes scenes.

# A.27 — CAMERA LANGUAGE
- Eye-level = normal conversation
- Low angle = power/victory/transformation
- High angle = weakness/defeat/overwhelm
- Extreme wide = GLX vs enormous forces
- Close-up = psychology/realization
- Over-shoulder = analysis/teaching
- Fish-eye/warped = chaos/comedy
- Dutch = instability/Glitch

# A.28 — SCALE LAW
Market forces should frequently dwarf GLX: giant candles, bulls/bears, waves, clocks, banks/cities, currency symbols. The Market remains larger than the protagonist.

# A.29 — BACKGROUND DENSITY
Caricature = high detail. Anime = medium action-focused detail. Chibi = minimal. Cinematic = high atmospheric detail with controlled composition.

# A.30 — EASTER EGG RULE
Use 1–3 meaningful hidden details in lore-heavy scenes: three-headed silhouette, CEREBUS glyph, GLITCH coordinates, broken clock, unopened vault, recurring number, mysterious door.

# A.31 — CONSISTENCY RED FLAGS
Reject/correct if hat is wrong color, loses fuzz, shades disappear without reason, skin tone shifts heavily, face becomes unrecognizable, beard changes randomly, body becomes overly muscular, clothing gets too colorful, chain disappears repeatedly, branding becomes excessive, character ages significantly, hat becomes tiny, anime/chibi/cinematic version looks like a different person.

# A.32 — FIVE-SECOND TEST
> **If logo and text disappeared, would a GLX follower recognize him within five seconds?**

If not, the model is too generic.

# A.33 — CANONICAL MODEL PROMPT CORE
> Young Black male GLX protagonist with medium-dark warm brown skin, recognizable short facial hair, oversized hot-pink fuzzy bucket hat, sleek black rectangular sunglasses, gold chain, black GLX streetwear, relaxed confident posture, expressive hands, youthful face, stylized but consistent likeness.

Add style after identity; never reinvent the character.

# A.34 — VISUAL SIGNATURE
> **Pink fuzz + black glass + gold + dark silhouette + market/glitch language.**

# A.35 — REFERENCE SHEET REQUIREMENT
Before final visual freeze, create front, 3/4, side, full body, neutral, smile, angry, shocked, analytical, exhausted, plus caricature, anime, chibi and cinematic model references.

# BLOCK 1A CANON SUMMARY
GLX must remain recognizable across all styles through oversized hot-pink fuzzy hat, sleek black shades, consistent face/facial hair, gold chain, black wardrobe, relaxed posture, expressive hands, warm medium-dark skin and glitch/market language.

> **Identity must survive the style.**
