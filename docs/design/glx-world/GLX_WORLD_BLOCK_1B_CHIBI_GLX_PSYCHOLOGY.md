# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 1B: CHIBI GLX PSYCHOLOGY

## Status
Canonical draft for the Inner World emotional sub-character system.

## Purpose
Block 1B defines Chibi GLX as more than a cute alternate style. He is the visible emotional operating system of GLX: impulse, fear, greed, excitement, doubt, curiosity, fatigue, confidence, and internal conflict.

# B.1 — WHAT CHIBI GLX ACTUALLY IS

> **The emotional operating system inside GLX.**

Chibi represents everything Outer GLX experiences internally but does not always express: fear, greed, excitement, impatience, ego, confidence, embarrassment, exhaustion, hope, anger, curiosity, celebration, doubt, boredom, temptation.

Outer GLX is the operator. Chibi GLX is the impulse system.

# B.2 — CHIBI IS ALWAYS THERE
Even when unseen, Chibi exists somewhere in the Inner World: watching, sleeping, panicking, scheming, celebrating, arguing, studying, remembering, trying to hijack controls, hiding or overreacting.

# B.3 — THE INNER CONTROL ROOM
Chibi's recurring headquarters:

> **The Control Room**

Potential elements:
- tiny trading desk
- giant BUY / SELL buttons
- emergency lever
- FEAR alarm
- GREED meter
- patience timer
- risk gauge
- dopamine gauge
- confidence meter
- DO NOT TOUCH controls
- discipline locks
- CEREBUS alert panel

# B.4 — CONTROL HIERARCHY
Outer GLX normally has control. Chibi can advise, complain, scream, warn, distract and celebrate.

Under pressure Chibi may attempt:

> **CONTROL HIJACK**

Typical triggers: FOMO, revenge trading, overconfidence, panic exits, moving stops, oversizing, chasing, breaking process.

# B.5 — CONTROL HIJACK VISUAL
Alarms flash, lights turn red, Chibi jumps into the main chair, discipline systems lock/fail, CEREBUS sends warnings, and Outer GLX begins behaving irrationally.

Psychology becomes visible storytelling.

# B.6 — CORE CHIBI PERSONALITY
- Hyper-emotional
- Impatient
- Curious
- Competitive
- Dramatic
- Optimistic
- Easily distracted
- Loyal

Chibi is not an enemy. He is emotion without enough regulation.

# B.7 — CHIBI IS NOT STUPID
Chibi can be reckless without being unintelligent. Sometimes he notices what Outer GLX ignores and can represent:

> **intuition before it has language**

# B.8 — THE SEVEN PRIMARY STATES

## 1. HYPE CHIBI
Triggers: wins, breakouts, TP, payout, breakthrough.
Behavior: dancing, screaming, confetti, glowing shades, riding candles.
Danger: overconfidence.

## 2. FEAR CHIBI
Triggers: drawdown, volatility, trade moving against him, uncertainty.
Behavior: sweating, hiding, clutching hat, repeated checking, catastrophizing.
Danger: premature exits/freeze.

## 3. GREED CHIBI
Triggers: large open profit, winning streak, explosive trend.
Behavior: dollar eyes, calculator, raising targets, grabbing leverage lever.
Danger: rational management becomes fantasy.

## 4. RAGE CHIBI
Triggers: stop then reversal, missed TP, slippage, bad execution, platform failure, one-pip miss.
Behavior: keyboard throw, chart punch, monitor bite, steam, tiny anime aura.
Danger: revenge trading.

## 5. SLEEPY CHIBI
Triggers: Asian session, late research, long backtest, inactivity.
Behavior: sleeping on keyboard, blanket, drooling, hat as pillow.
Danger: fatigue and poor judgment.

## 6. DETECTIVE CHIBI
Triggers: anomalies, strange correlation, hidden pattern.
Behavior: magnifying glass, conspiracy board, notebook, strings, trench coat.
Use: useful curiosity.

## 7. ZEN CHIBI
Rare. Triggered by discipline, clarity, acceptance and controlled execution.
Behavior: meditation, tea, candles moving without reaction.
Zen becomes more common as GLX matures.

# B.9 — SECONDARY STATES
Confused, Suspicious, Rich, Broke, Sick, Hungry, Coding, Teacher, Student, Payout, Analyst, Smug, Ghost, Burnout, FOMO, Locked-In.

These power the sticker/reaction library.

# B.10 — EMOTION COMBINATIONS
Fear + Greed: one hand on CLOSE, one on ADD POSITION.
Hype + Ego: Chibi wears a crown and acts like he is the Market.
Rage + FOMO: missed entry becomes chase impulse.
Sleepy + Greed: half asleep but refuses to take profit.
Detective + Obsession: useful research becomes conspiracy-room chaos.

# B.11 — IMPULSE METER
0 Calm
1 Noticed
2 Active
3 Loud
4 Interfering
5 Hijack Risk
6 Control Hijack

Recurring visual language for emotions like FOMO.

# B.12 — DISCIPLINE SYSTEM
Inside the Control Room, **DISCIPLINE** locks certain controls.

Examples:
- BUY: LOCKED UNTIL ENTRY
- Leverage: LIMITED
- Revenge Trade: ACCESS DENIED

As GLX matures, Discipline becomes more reliable.

# B.13 — EARLY INNER WORLD
Chaotic: too many buttons, alarms, broken monitors, snacks, coffee, contradictory indicators, Chibi screaming.

# B.14 — ADVANCED INNER WORLD
Cleaner: fewer controls, stronger risk dashboard, calmer Chibi, fewer alarms, more automation, more space.

Psychological development becomes environmental development.

# B.15 — CHIBI AND CEREBUS
CEREBUS is hyper-logical; Chibi hyper-emotional.

Example:
CEREBUS: “Expected value remains unchanged.”
Chibi: “BRO WE ARE DOWN RIGHT NOW.”
CEREBUS: “Correct.”
Chibi: “I HATE YOU.”

Over time they become a stronger team: CEREBUS learns human context; Chibi learns probability.

# B.16 — CHIBI AND FEAR
Early, Fear manifests through Chibi. Later Fear may become a distinct entity. A major growth moment is Chibi realizing:

> “That voice isn't always me.”

# B.17 — CHIBI AND GREED
Same progression. Greed may eventually become a separate entity whispering things like “Just one more target.” Chibi can agree, resist, bargain or learn.

# B.18 — AUDIENCE SURROGATE
Chibi can react exactly like the viewer.

Quant GLX gets too technical.
Chibi: “English please.”
GLX simplifies.

# B.19 — FOURTH-WALL RULE
Outer GLX generally stays inside the universe. Chibi may occasionally acknowledge the audience, especially for meme timing.

# B.20 — REACTION TIMING
Preferred comedy grammar:

> **Setup → Serious Moment → Pause → Chibi Reaction**

# B.21 — CHIBI DOES NOT ALWAYS SPEAK
Strong silent reactions include slow blink, blank stare, tiny tear, trembling, reaching toward BUY, hiding behind monitor, lying face down.

# B.22 — PHYSICAL COMEDY
Chibi may flatten, stretch, explode, turn ghost, shrink, become giant, melt, catch fire, freeze, vibrate, spin or launch through walls—but the gag must reflect emotion.

# B.23 — HAT PHYSICS

Anger = hat catches fire.
Greed = gold symbols in fuzz.
Sleep = pillow.
Glitch = pixelation.
Victory = absurd growth.

# B.24 — GLASSES PHYSICS
Greed = dollar signs.
Love = hearts.

Locked-In = screen reflections.

# B.25 — FAILURE LOOP
Recurring comedy:
1. Chibi predicts disaster.
2. Outer follows process.
3. Trade works.
4. Chibi celebrates like he knew it.

Or Chibi screams to enter, Outer ignores him, trade fails, and Chibi pretends he never wanted it.

# B.26 — CHIBI LEARNING
Early: “PRICE MOVING = TRADE.”
Later: “Do we have confirmation?”
Advanced: “What's risk?”
Eventually Chibi can stop Outer GLX from doing something reckless.

# B.27 — MEMORY VAULT
The Inner World contains a Memory Vault storing losses, humiliations, missed opportunities, wins, mistakes and breakthroughs. Present emotion can be triggered by old events.

# B.28 — TRAUMA VS DATA
A bad experience can become either **Trauma** or **Data** depending on how GLX processes it.

Unprocessed memory becomes monster.
Processed memory becomes file.

> **The Lab converts emotional memory into structured learning.**

# B.29 — CONFIDENCE
Healthy: “We've trained this. Follow the process.”
Unhealthy: “We literally cannot lose.”

Healthy Chibi straps in calmly. Ego Chibi wears a crown and cape.

# B.30 — REAL PURPOSE
> **Knowing better and feeling better are not the same thing.**

That gap is where Chibi lives.

# B.31 — CONTENT ROLES
Reaction stickers, trading memes, psychology education, Discord reactions, comments, transitions, inner monologues, audience-surrogate moments, running jokes and emotional storytelling.

# B.32 — MOST IMPORTANT RULE
The goal is not to eliminate Chibi/emotion.

> **Understand emotion without surrendering control to it.**

At the end of GLX's arc, Chibi still exists—but sits beside the controls instead of stealing them.

# B.33 — END-STATE CHIBI
Early: reacts to everything.
Intermediate: recognizes reactions.
Advanced: communicates them.
Master: feels fully without automatically acting.

# BLOCK 1B CANON SUMMARY
Chibi GLX is:

> **GLX's emotional operating system made visible.**

Primary states: Hype, Fear, Greed, Rage, Sleepy, Detective, Zen.

Key conflict:
> **Impulse vs Process**

Evolution:
> **Reaction → Awareness → Regulation → Partnership**

Deepest rule:
> **Emotion is not the enemy. Unexamined emotion controlling behavior is.**
