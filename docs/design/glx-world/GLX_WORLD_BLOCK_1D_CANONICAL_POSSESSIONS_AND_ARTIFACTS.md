# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 1D: CANONICAL POSSESSIONS & ARTIFACTS

## Status
Canonical draft for recurring GLX objects, tools, weapons, interfaces, and lore artifacts.

## Purpose
Block 1D makes GLX WORLD tactile. Important recurring objects should have a function, visual identity, relationship to GLX, symbolic meaning, and story value.

# D.1 — CORE PRINCIPLE
GLX should not carry random props just because a scene needs detail.

Important objects should reveal:
- who GLX is
- how GLX thinks
- what GLX learned
- what GLX is building
- what GLX is becoming

If somebody sees the pink hat beside a black notebook and a glowing CEREBUS terminal, they should know whose world they are in.

# D.2 — THE FIELD BOOK
Canonical working name:

> **The Field Book**

A matte-black physical notebook with subtle GLX mark, worn corners, pink binding accent, handwritten diagrams, chart sketches, arrows, failed ideas, dates, and tiny Chibi doodles.

Represents:
> **thinking before certainty**

# D.3 — WHY IT MATTERS
Computers contain data. CEREBUS contains analysis. The Field Book contains:

> **GLX's evolving understanding.**

Questions, hypotheses, intuition, half-formed models, observations, mistakes and unexplained patterns live here before formalization.

# D.4 — FIELD BOOK PROGRESSION
Early: messy, arrows, stains, random calculations, unfinished thoughts.
Intermediate: structured, indexed, categorized.
Ascendant: minimal, high-signal, few notes.

# D.5 — THE BLACK MUG
Matte black, gold/pink detail, sturdy, slightly oversized.

Primary inscription:
> **PROCESS > PREDICTION**

# D.6 — MUG COMEDY
Can act as silent timing prop: Lab exploding while GLX calmly sips; Glitch begins and the mug starts floating; GLX: “...not again.”

# D.7 — THE GLX COMMAND DESK
Canonical workstation, not just “many monitors.”

Core structure:
- large dark desk
- curved multi-monitor arrangement
- central primary screen
- side research displays
- notebook area
- CEREBUS interface position
- keyboard/stylus/tablet
- mug position
- subtle pink light
- gold accents

# D.8 — DESK ZONES
## CENTER — EXECUTION
Primary chart, clean, few distractions.

## LEFT — RESEARCH
Data, backtests, charts, docs, models.

## RIGHT — CEREBUS
Interface, alerts, system state, assistant functions.

## PHYSICAL DESK — HUMAN LAYER
Field Book, pen, mug, phone, physical notes.

> **Human intuition + machine intelligence + execution**

# D.9 — DESK STATE CHANGES
Normal = organized/lived-in.
Quant = research side expands.
Warrior = center screen dominates.
Macro = screens merge into global environment.
Broken = black screens, cold mug, closed notebook.
Ascendant = fewer screens, cleaner space.

# D.10 — GLX PEN
Black-and-gold pen/stylus hybrid. Writes in Field Book, becomes digital stylus in Quant, writes on boards in Mentor, and can conceptually become a Market World blade.

> **The weapon begins as a tool for understanding.**

# D.11 — THE CANDLE BLADE
Warrior GLX primary weapon. Not a generic katana. Its structure references a candlestick with wick-like extensions and a market-energy core.

Bullish and bearish structures may alter energy flow, but GLX owns the weapon independent of direction.

# D.12 — CANDLE BLADE PHILOSOPHY
> **execution through price structure**

It does not predict. It responds. When structure disappears, the blade becomes unstable. When setup is clear, the blade sharpens.

# D.13 — DUAL BLADES
For two-sided/choppy markets: one blade represents bull structure, the other bear structure.

> **“I don't need to marry a direction.”**

# D.14 — THE RISK SHIELD
Translucent black/red energy, percentage markings, boundary grid, finite capacity.

Represents stop loss, sizing, invalidation and exposure limits.

> **The shield has finite capacity.**

Oversizing weakens it. Recklessness cracks it.

# D.15 — THE LIQUIDITY RIFLE
Precision weapon representing patience, location, defined entry, waiting for price, and high-RR execution.

Visual: sleek black rifle with pink/glitch accents; scope contains market geometry.

Best for limit entries, rekeys, liquidity zones and sniper setups.

# D.16 — RIFLE RULE
Most powerful when location, risk and target are defined and patience is maintained. FOMO distorts scope. Impatience reduces accuracy.

# D.17 — FIB EQUIPMENT
Specialized tools such as segmented blades, throwing markers or geometry projectors, potentially marked 50/72/82/132/168/185.

> **Specialized artifact = specialized concept**

Only appears when the content actually concerns Fib geometry.

# D.18 — THE FLOW ATLAS
Macro GLX signature object: a holographic globe/map showing currencies, commodities, volatility, capital flows, rates, risk, sessions and cross-asset relationships.

It does not show “the answer.” It shows relationships.

# D.19 — FLOW ATLAS VISUAL
Dark globe, gold continental lines, floating currencies, pink/blue flow streams, oil routes, dollar routes, yield paths. Destabilization shifts routes.

# D.20 — THE CEREBUS TERMINAL
CEREBUS first appears through a physical interface before full entity reveal.

Early audience sees a dark terminal, three circular indicators, strange glyph, terse responses, black screen and red/pink light.

# D.21 — THREE-LIGHT MOTIF
Recurring identifier:

> **Three lights**

May appear on terminal, doors, Lab, Glitch events or reflected in GLX glasses. Meaning stays unexplained early.

# D.22 — THE CORE KEY
Deep-lore access object: black metallic token, three-node symbol, pink/gold internal glow, handheld size. Used for deeper CEREBUS access and never shown casually.

# D.23 — THE ARCHIVE
Physical/digital hybrid containing CEREBUS Files, failed experiments, historical events, old strategies, Field Books, charts, research, recordings and damaged artifacts.

Visual feel:
> **library + server room + classified archive**

# D.24 — FILE CLASSIFICATION
- OBSERVATION — interesting but untested
- HYPOTHESIS — testable idea
- VALIDATED — supported by evidence
- REJECTED — failed testing
- ANOMALY — unexplained
- GLITCH — does not fit current framework

This can become actual content packaging.

# D.25 — REJECTED FILES MATTER
The Archive openly keeps failed ideas.

Chibi sees enormous REJECTED section: “Damn.”
GLX: “That's why the good stuff works.”

> **failure produces information**

# D.26 — THE MARKET CLOCK
Specialized time device showing Asian, London, New York, overlaps, closes and transitions.

Outer World = compact Lab display.
Market World = enormous architecture.

# D.27 — CLOCK DISTORTION
During volatility/Glitch: hands reverse, sessions overlap, clock fragments, time stretches, transitions desynchronize. Prepares Time Layer lore.

# D.28 — THE RISK COIN
Black/gold coin: one side **RISK**, other **REWARD**.

Represents inseparability of reward and exposure.

# D.29 — NO GAMBLING RULE
GLX never uses the Risk Coin to choose trades except when explicitly mocking bad trading.

# D.30 — GLX HEADPHONES
Black over-ear headphones with subtle pink ring, used for Quant mode, deep work and late research.

Recurring joke: headphones on → Chibi realizes “Oh, we finna be here for six hours.”

# D.31 — THE PHONE
Primarily symbolizes external interruption: Discord, news, alerts, messages. Chibi wants to check it; GLX may flip it face-down when entering focus.

# D.32 — THE BLACK CASE
Outer World: technical equipment case near the desk.
Market World: opens into armory.

Potential contents: Candle Blade, Liquidity Rifle, Fib tools, Risk Shield generator.

# D.33 — ARMORY RULE
Artifacts are earned conceptually. When GLX genuinely develops or masters a new real framework, its fictional equivalent may be added to the Black Case.

This bridges real research and fictional lore.

# D.34 — ARTIFACT EVOLUTION
Artifacts upgrade alongside GLX.

Example Risk Shield:
- Early — manual, clunky, cracks easily
- Later — lighter, integrated, efficient
- Ascendant — barely visible, internalized

Field Book, Candle Blade, CEREBUS Terminal, Flow Atlas and Command Desk can evolve similarly.

# D.35 — ARTIFACT DAMAGE
Objects preserve history: chipped Candle Blade, cracked sunglasses archived after drawdown, old Field Books, first CEREBUS Terminal, damaged Risk Shield.

The universe remembers its past.

# D.36 — OBJECT MEMORY
Objects may trigger flashbacks: old notebook → first discovery; broken shades → drawdown era; old Risk Shield → reckless phase.

## PERSONAL
Hat, shades, chain, Field Book, mug, pen, headphones, Risk Coin.

## SYSTEM
Command Desk, CEREBUS Terminal, Flow Atlas, Market Clock, Black Case, Archive.

Personal reveals character. System reveals capability.

Before everything:

GLX.
A chart.
A pen.
A notebook.

> **the Builder writing down what he noticed**
