# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 2.1: COSMOLOGY & WORLD STRUCTURE

## Status
Canonical draft for layered reality, world physics, Market Entry, Glitch behavior, market depth, and cross-layer logic.

## Core Cosmology
GLX WORLD is layered, not separated. The Outer World, Market World, and Inner World are different layers of the same reality.

### Outer World
What ordinary people perceive: charts, screens, news, money, trades, computers, cities, and people.

### Market World
The hidden structure of market behavior made visible: liquidity, risk, volatility, capital flows, sessions, structure, institutions, positioning, and regime.

### Inner World
The internal human experience of interacting with those forces: fear, greed, memory, confidence, discipline, impulse, intuition, fatigue, and identity.

One event may occur in all three layers simultaneously.

> **Physical Reality → Market Reality → Psychological Reality**

A EURUSD spike can be a candle in Outer World, a violent liquidity current in Market World, and a Fear alarm inside Inner World.

---

# 2.1.1 — THE VEIL

The boundary between ordinary perception and Market World is **The Veil**. It is not necessarily a physical wall. It represents the limitation of normal perception.

Most people see price. GLX increasingly learns to see structure beneath price.

The Veil becomes thinner around trading screens, exchanges, the Quant Lab, major market opens, high-volatility events, central-bank decisions, liquidity shocks, CEREBUS systems, and Glitch zones.

---

# 2.1.2 — MARKET ENTRY

Market Entry is **perceptual synchronization with the Market layer**. GLX does not normally vanish physically from the Outer World. Anime/Warrior GLX visualizes what he perceives beneath ordinary price.

Canonically, Market World is neither purely physical nor merely imagination. It is a real informational layer experienced through perception.

---

# 2.1.3 — MARKET WORLD PHYSICS

## Liquidity = Water
Flows, collects, drains, pools, surges, and forms tides.

## Volatility = Weather
Appears as calm, pressure, storms, lightning, fronts, and sudden changes.

## Risk = Exposure
Appears as damage potential, boundaries, shield capacity, unstable ground, and hazardous zones.

## Capital = Energy
Flows, concentrates, powers systems, moves infrastructure, and empowers participants.

## Time = Terrain
Some routes, doors, and opportunities exist only during certain sessions.

## Price = Position
Price represents where current state exists relative to structure. Price does not need to become a character.

---

# 2.1.4 — CAUSAL CONSISTENCY

Fantasy events must correspond to meaningful financial, psychological, or narrative structure.

A wave should mean liquidity or volatility. A bridge collapse should mean structural failure. A Bull breaking a wall should mean resistance being breached. Water draining should mean liquidity withdrawal.

> **The fantasy layer is a translation system, not random decoration.**

---

# 2.1.5 — CROSS-LAYER FEEDBACK

The layers influence one another.

Market volatility rises → Inner Fear rises → Outer GLX changes behavior → his action changes his Market exposure.

The Inner World therefore matters because internal state changes external decision quality.

---

# 2.1.6 — THE GLITCH

A Glitch occurs when layers stop remaining cleanly separated. Market objects may appear in Outer World, Inner emotion may distort Market perception, CEREBUS may perceive across layers, time may behave incorrectly, or GLX may see multiple realities simultaneously.

> **The Glitch is cross-layer leakage.**

Glitch types:
- Natural Glitch — extreme market condition or rare structural alignment.
- CEREBUS Glitch — deep computation/synchronization side effect.
- Psychological Glitch — emotional or cognitive distortion.
- Unknown Glitch — unexplained deep-lore anomaly.

False Glitches matter. Ego, fear, fatigue, obsession, or confirmation bias can look like hidden structure. CEREBUS may detect no actual anomaly.

---

# 2.1.7 — MARKET DEPTH

## Surface Market
Visible price movement and normal session behavior.

## Structural Market
Liquidity, volatility, flows, positioning, correlation, intermarket relationships, and regime. This is GLX's primary advanced operating layer.

## Deep Market
Institutional-scale flows, unknown interactions, Deep Liquidity, temporal structures, Market Core, and other poorly understood systems.

Depth is not a power level. Depth means less certainty and greater information pressure.

---

# 2.1.8 — DEPTH VISUAL LAW

Surface Market uses recognizable cities, roads, terrain, and landmarks.

Structural Market becomes more abstract: currents, grids, bridges, large systems, and dynamic relationships.

Deep Market uses impossible geometry, dark oceans, enormous structures, unstable perspective, minimal landmarks, and distorted time.

---

# 2.1.9 — INFORMATION PRESSURE

Deeper perception increases information pressure and may create Noise Saturation, Glitch overload, fatigue, sensory distortion, Ego Distortion, or forced disconnect.

CEREBUS helps filter. It does not remove uncertainty.

---

# 2.1.10 — ACCESS LIMITS

Possible hierarchy:
- Ordinary people — Outer World.
- Traders — partial Market perception.
- Experienced traders — Surface Market awareness.
- GLX — Structural Market access.
- CEREBUS — deeper structural processing.
- Glitched GLX — temporary Deep Market access.
- Ascendant GLX — greater stability at depth.
- Unknown entities — possibly native to deeper layers.

Understanding, not arbitrary combat strength, determines access.

---

# 2.1.11 — THE MARKET HAS NO SIMPLE THRONE

The Market is distributed. Even if a Market Core exists, it is not a single ruler controlling all outcomes. The system itself is the force.

---

# 2.1.12 — SESSION TRANSITIONS

Sessions change the world physically. Asia → London can mean blue night brightening, quiet districts waking, liquidity channels widening, volatility clouds gathering, and London Gates opening.

London/New York overlap can become a temporary merged geography with increased activity, liquidity, opportunity, and danger.

When sessions close, some roads shut, lights go dark, currents change, and populations move.

---

# 2.1.13 — MARKET EVENTS AS WORLD EVENTS

Examples:
- CPI — shockwave.
- NFP — violent storm front.
- Central-bank decision — signal tower or bell alters rates/gravity.
- Intervention — external force enters a realm.
- Liquidity crisis — water disappears or relocates violently.
- Flash crash — terrain collapses.
- Carry unwind — capital rivers reverse.

Detailed mythology belongs to Block 4.

# CORE WORLD LAW

> **Nothing in GLX WORLD is metaphorical by accident.**

Rain, water, weapons, cities, lights, animals, doors, and time should carry financial, psychological, or narrative meaning.

## Canon Summary
Outer World shows what happens. Market World shows what is happening underneath. Inner World shows what it does to the human operator. The Glitch appears when layers leak. Deeper layers increase both possible insight and danger of misinterpretation.
