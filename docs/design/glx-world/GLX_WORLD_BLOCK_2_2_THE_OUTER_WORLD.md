# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 2.2: THE OUTER WORLD

## Status
Canonical draft for the grounded reality of GLX WORLD and its recurring physical locations.

## Core Identity
The Outer World is the believable human layer of GLX WORLD. Its central recurring environment is the **GLX Quant Lab**, a physical manifestation of the Builder-Explorer.

The Lab should feel like:

> **trading desk + research lab + workshop + classified archive + creative studio**

It must remain lived-in rather than sterile: Field Books, charts, coffee, prototypes, failed ideas, old hardware, repairs, and Chibi chaos.

---

# 2.2.1 — QUANT LAB ARCHITECTURE

Core zones:
- Command Floor
- Research Bay
- CEREBUS Chamber
- Archive
- Workshop
- Observation Deck
- Training Wing
- Glitch Containment Zone

Every room represents a different function of GLX.

---

# 2.2.2 — COMMAND FLOOR

Primary recurring location and home of the GLX Command Desk.

Visual hierarchy:
1. GLX
2. primary chart / current problem
3. CEREBUS state
4. relevant research
5. atmosphere

The Command Floor is a thin-Veil location and the normal point of Market Entry.

Avoid meaningless hologram clutter. Screens should communicate actual price, risk, sessions, models, correlations, anomalies, or system state.

---

# 2.2.3 — RESEARCH BAY

Quant GLX territory.

Contains:
- whiteboards,
- test results,
- books,
- terminals,
- data walls,
- simulations,
- printed research,
- discarded hypotheses.

This is where:

> “I noticed something weird.”

becomes:

> “Can we prove it?”

Canonical feature: **The Hypothesis Wall** with OBSERVATION / HYPOTHESIS / TESTING / VALIDATED / REJECTED / ANOMALY states.

Failed ideas remain visible. Failure produces information.

---

# 2.2.4 — CEREBUS CHAMBER

Quieter, darker, more controlled than the rest of the Lab.

Early visual language:
- CEREBUS Terminal,
- three-light motif,
- black surfaces,
- minimal interface,
- low mechanical hum,
- unexplained sealed architecture.

Used for advanced analysis, anomaly review, model synchronization, deep computation, and classified research.

Rare uses include Glitch events, Core Key access, deep-layer synchronization, and major CEREBUS evolution.

---

# 2.2.5 — THE ARCHIVE

The Archive represents **memory**.

Visual:
- tall shelves,
- dark cabinets,
- server racks,
- Field Books,
- old hardware,
- preserved artifacts,
- warm low light.

Possible zones:
- Field Books,
- CEREBUS Files,
- Rejected Models,
- Historical Events,
- Damaged Artifacts,
- Unknown Files,
- Sealed Archive.

The Archive should visibly preserve mistakes and historical continuity.

---

# 2.2.6 — THE WORKSHOP

The Workshop converts real system development into fictional artifacts.

Outer World development can appear as code, prototypes, strategy documents, hardware, or new risk systems.

Market World translation can become Candle Blade upgrades, Liquidity Rifle scopes, Risk Shield improvements, or specialized Fib equipment.

> **Every important fictional artifact should have an Outer World origin.**

---

# 2.2.7 — OBSERVATION DECK

Macro GLX territory.

Contains:
- Flow Atlas,
- world clocks,
- currencies,
- commodities,
- rates,
- global market displays,
- a city view that can transform into visible capital flows.

Purpose:

> **context before decision**

# 2.2.8 — TRAINING WING

Mentor GLX territory.

Used for:
- teaching,
- simulation,
- replay,
- historical reconstruction,
- practice,
- community/mentorship scenes.

Historical events can be loaded into a simulation, allowing educational content to become training missions.

---

# 2.2.9 — GLITCH CONTAINMENT ZONE

Rare-use area with heavy doors, three-light motifs, shielding, unstable screens, and recovered anomalous objects.

Its purpose should remain partially unexplained early.

---

# 2.2.10 — GLX HOME

Home exists to preserve humanity outside the Lab.

Used for:
- sleep,
- food,
- relationships,
- exhaustion,
- late-night checking,
- recovery,
- ambition,
- relatable comedy.

Less neon, more natural light. Small GLX anchors keep it recognizable.

---

# 2.2.11 — THE OUTER CITY

A grounded broader city where GLX can travel, eat, walk, interact, and experience ordinary life.

Rare Market bleed can occur here: impossible reflections, Bull silhouettes, liquidity where no water should be, clocks skipping, or symbols only GLX notices.

If everything is surreal all the time, Glitches lose meaning.

Target tone:
- 70% believable,
- 20% heightened,
- 10% strange.

---

# 2.2.12 — THE BLACK DOOR

Canonical long-term mystery inside the Lab:

> **The Black Door**

No label. No obvious handle. Three small lights above it. GLX does not explain it.

Its destination is intentionally unresolved. Possible future connections include the CEREBUS Core, a Glitch Corridor, or Deep Market access.

Do not decide everything early.

---

# 2.2.13 — LAB EVOLUTION

Early Lab:
- small,
- messy,
- inexpensive,
- idea-dense.

Builder Era:
- more rooms,
- growing Archive,
- CEREBUS Chamber emerges.

Operator Era:
- cleaner,
- specialized,
- less clutter.

Architect Era:
- major research facility.

Ascendant Era:
- simpler,
- quieter,
- deeply integrated.

> **Mastery reduces visible noise.**

---

# 2.2.14 — PERSISTENT CONSEQUENCE

The Lab should remember its history:
- scorch marks,
- repaired walls,
- broken screens,
- old hardware,
- archived prototypes,
- damaged artifacts.

The environment should not reset after each episode.

---

# CANON SUMMARY

The Quant Lab is the primary Outer World stage.

Command Floor = execution.
Research Bay = inquiry.
CEREBUS Chamber = machine intelligence.
Archive = memory.
Workshop = creation.
Observation Deck = perspective.
Training Wing = transmission.
Glitch Zone = unknown.
Home = humanity.

The Lab evolves alongside GLX and should eventually feel like a character itself.
