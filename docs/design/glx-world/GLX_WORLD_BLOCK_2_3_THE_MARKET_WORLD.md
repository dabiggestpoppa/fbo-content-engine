# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 2.3: THE MARKET WORLD

## Status
Canonical draft for the primary fantasy/financial geography of GLX WORLD.

## Purpose
The Market World is the visible expression of hidden market structure. It converts sessions, liquidity, volatility, capital, correlations, asset classes, risk, and regime into geography, weather, ecology, infrastructure, and civilization.

It must function at four levels:
1. fantasy,
2. intuition,
3. market mechanics,
4. CEREBUS ontology.

> **Nothing is metaphorical by accident.**

# 2.3.2 — LIQUIDITY OCEAN

Liquidity becomes water. The Liquidity Ocean functions as the circulatory system of Market World.

It contains currents, pools, tides, channels, whirlpools, dry zones, deep trenches, and violent surges.

Large concentrations of resting liquidity become Pools. Sweeps become violent current events. Deep Liquidity exists below ordinary visibility and behaves more like pressure than surface flow.

Core identity:

Mood:
- moonlit,
- quiet,
- precise,
- contained,
- patient.

Asia establishes range, early structure, stored liquidity, positioning, and conditions inherited by later sessions.

# 2.3.6 — COLLISION ZONE

The London/New York overlap becomes:

> **The Collision Zone**

It is a temporary superposition of two session civilizations: London stone through New York glass, fog through neon, old bridges through capital grids.

It represents maximum participation, competing interpretation, rapid repricing, opportunity, and danger.

---

# 2.3.7 — DOLLAR EMPIRE

Core identity:

> **The Gravity Well of Global Finance**

The Dollar Empire represents structural USD centrality, not moral judgment.

Features:
- Reserve Vaults,
- rate towers,
- Treasury roads,
- funding pipelines,
- global bridges.

Dollar strength becomes **Dollar Gravity**. When gravity intensifies, global routes bend toward the Empire. When it relaxes, capital disperses.

# 2.3.8 — FX CROSSROADS

The broader FX world is organized into currency Houses and cross-border corridors. Possible Houses include Dollar, Euro, Sterling, Yen, Franc, and commodity-linked currencies.

Pairs are roads, bridges, and border relationships rather than dozens of unrelated mascots.

# 2.3.13 — VOLATILITY FIELDS

Volatility becomes weather.

The Fields contain pressure systems, storms, lightning, wind, terrain change, and visibility shifts. Storms can move between regions, making contagion intuitive.

---

# 2.3.14 — RISK ZONES

Risk is a condition, not a country.

Risk Zones may appear as leverage cliffs, unstable bridges, event blast zones, red exposure fields, or thin-liquidity deserts.

---

# 2.3.15 — INTERMARKET BRIDGES

Relationships between markets become bridges.

Examples:
- Oil ↔ CAD,
- Gold ↔ USD,
- Rates ↔ Dollar,
- Rates ↔ Equities,
- risk sentiment ↔ JPY/CHF.

Correlation breakdown becomes a cracking bridge. New regimes can create new bridges.

---

# 2.3.16 — CAPITAL HIGHWAYS

Large systemic flows travel along Capital Highways linking the Dollar Empire, Equity Kingdoms, Commodity Lands, FX territories, and Sanctuary Routes.

Traffic intensity represents flow. Congestion represents crowding.

---

# 2.3.17 — SANCTUARY ROUTES

Under specific stress regimes, conditional routes toward defensive assets may activate: Yen territories, Franc territories, Gold Highlands, or Treasury infrastructure.

They are not permanently safe.

---

# 2.3.18 — MARKET ECOLOGY

Market World behaves as an ecosystem.

High volatility + thin liquidity can create predatory conditions. Low volatility + deep liquidity can create stable conditions. Crowded positioning + leverage can create liquidation conditions. Strong macro flow + deep capital can create sustained currents.

CEREBUS classifies states. GLX experiences ecology.

---

# 2.3.19 — MARKET FAUNA

Possible functional fauna:
- Pip Birds — movement/activity.
- Stopfish — liquidity concentration.
- Spread Eels — widening spread in thin conditions.
- Volatility Rays — expansion pressure.
- Leverage Leeches — excess exposure.

> **Every creature must represent real behavior, condition, risk, participant behavior, or information.**

---

# 2.3.20 — BULL & BEAR

Bull and Bear are ancient directional archetypes.

Bull = sustained upward force.
Bear = sustained downward force.

They are not good and evil and they are not gods. Broad directional participation may appear as Herd Events.

---

# 2.3.21 — POSITIONING AS POPULATION

Crowded positioning appears as crowded geography: packed streets, overloaded bridges, ships facing one direction, or banners concentrated on one side.

A reversal becomes a stampede.

---

# 2.3.22 — INFORMATION & UNCERTAINTY

Canonical metaphors:

> **Information = Light**

> **Uncertainty = Fog**

Reliable information illuminates. False information flickers. CEREBUS can reduce fog, never eliminate it.

---

# 2.3.23 — EDGE AS NAVIGATION

Edge is not magical attack power.

> **Edge is the ability to navigate Market World better than average.**

It may come from better maps, timing, tools, weather awareness, or risk control.

---

# 2.3.24 — RISK AS THE PRICE OF MOVEMENT

> **You cannot move through Market World without exposure.**

Every opportunity requires accepting uncertainty.

---

# 2.3.25 — MARKET MEMORY

GLX and CEREBUS continuously update the Market Atlas.

Every major Market World concept should pass two tests:

> **Would this still be interesting if the viewer knew nothing about markets?**

and:

## Canon Summary
Asia establishes. London tests. New York reprices. Liquidity connects. Volatility changes weather. Capital powers movement. Risk constrains movement. Information illuminates. Uncertainty obscures. Relationships become bridges. Regimes reshape the environment.

> **The Market World changes because the market changes.**
