# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 2.4: THE INNER WORLD

## Status
Canonical draft for the psychological geography of GLX WORLD.

## Purpose
The Market World explains how markets behave. The Inner World explains how humans behave while operating under uncertainty.

The Inner World is a living psychological civilization where cognition, emotion, memory, discipline, attention, identity, intuition, fatigue, confidence, bias, and values physically interact.

> **What GLX feels internally changes how he perceives externally.**

---

# 2.4.1 — NOT A CARTOON BRAIN

The Inner World is not literal anatomy. It should feel like a psychological city built around consciousness: part technology, part architecture, part memory, part dream-space.

---

# 2.4.2 — CONTROL DISTRICT

At the center is the **Control District** and its primary structure, the **Control Room**.

The Control Room contains:
- BUY / SELL controls,
- FEAR alarm,
- GREED meter,
- dopamine gauge,
- patience timer,
- risk display,
- confidence meter,
- emergency controls,
- Discipline locks,
- CEREBUS behavioral alerts.

It receives four principal feeds:
- External Feed — charts, news, P&L, phone, comments, physical state.
- Market Feed — liquidity, volatility, risk, structure, CEREBUS signals.
- Memory Feed — prior wins, losses, embarrassment, lessons, confidence.
- Emotion Feed — current Chibi state.

---

# 2.4.3 — OPERATOR CHAIR

The central seat is the **Operator Chair**.

Outer GLX is the rightful operator. Chibi can reach it. During Control Hijack, Chibi attempts to operate before Discipline intervenes.

The chair represents responsibility, not rulership.

---

# 2.4.4 — DISCIPLINE CORE

Beneath the Control District is the **Discipline Core**.

It powers:
- risk locks,
- patience controls,
- execution rules,
- access restrictions,
- emotional override protection,
- routine enforcement.

Discipline is infrastructure, not motivational speech.

Early GLX has an unstable Core. Broken GLX has a damaged Core. Ascendant GLX has deeply integrated, visually minimal discipline.

---

# 2.4.5 — HABIT PATHWAYS

Repeated behavior creates roads and shortcuts.

Good habits create efficient paths toward Strategy, Discipline, and Risk systems.

Bad habits create bypasses.

Canonical example:

> **FOMO Tunnel**

It moves Chibi from “price is moving” to execution before analysis is complete.

---

# 2.4.6 — MEMORY VAULT

The Memory Vault stores experience as files, rooms, objects, fragments, emotional tags, and reconstructed events.

Memory types:
- Procedural Memory — what do we normally do?
- Emotional Memory — how did this make us feel?
- Analytical Memory — what did we learn?
- Identity Memory — what do I believe about myself?

Memory is not objective. Emotional memory can exaggerate events.

---

# 2.4.7 — TRAUMA CHAMBERS

Unresolved emotionally charged experiences may form **Trauma Chambers**.

Possible sources include catastrophic rule violations, blown accounts, humiliation, painful missed opportunities, or identity collapse.

Use this language carefully and never trivialize serious mental-health experiences.

Canonical law:

> **Unprocessed experience becomes a monster. Processed experience becomes a file.**

---

# 2.4.8 — FEAR DISTRICT

Fear's environment is the **Fear District**.

Visual:
- warning towers,
- red emergency lights,
- shelters,
- defensive walls,
- sirens,
- threat maps.

Fear is not evil. It is a protective system that can become overactive.

Healthy Fear: “Check the risk.”
Unhealthy Fear: “Everything is dangerous.”

The district contains the **Threat Tower**, which detects danger and can become oversensitive.

---

# 2.4.9 — GOLDEN QUARTER

Greed's environment is the **Golden Quarter**.

Visual:
- gold light,
- enormous projections,
- expanding towers,
- leverage advertisements,
- impossible future wealth scenes.

Greed's core mechanism is turning possibility into perceived certainty.

Inside it sits the **Projection Palace**, which displays imagined outcomes and unrealized scenarios.

---

# 2.4.10 — SWING CORRIDOR

Fear District and Golden Quarter sit surprisingly close together, connected by **The Swing Corridor**.

The mind can move rapidly between:

> “Get me out.”

and:

> “Add more.”

---

# 2.4.11 — EGO TOWER

Ego occupies the **Ego Tower**.

Its apparent height changes with recent outcomes.

Ego represents self-image overriding evidence.

From the tower, Risk Zones look smaller, fog seems thinner, and paths appear more obvious than they really are.

Inside is **Confirmation Hall**, where supporting evidence is displayed while contradictory evidence is hidden.

---

# 2.4.12 — THE CROSSROADS

Doubt lives at **The Crossroads**.

Healthy Doubt:
> “Check the assumption.”

Unhealthy Doubt:
> “Never decide.”

Doubt is useful for Quant GLX, but Warrior GLX cannot remain trapped there forever.

---

# 2.4.13 — STRATEGY ROOM

The Strategy Room contains scenario branches, risk paths, invalidation markers, probability overlays, and decision trees.

Instead of “price must go up,” it displays Path A / B / C with conditions.

This is the visual home of conditional thinking.

---

# 2.4.14 — IMAGINATION DISTRICT

The **Imagination District** contains unfinished structures, floating ideas, impossible prototypes, doors to nowhere, and strange connections.

Creativity produces hypotheses, strategies, systems, stories, and models — but also fantasies, overfitting, and unsupported narratives.

Inside it is the **Idea Foundry**, where raw observations become candidate ideas.

---

# 2.4.15 — ATTENTION GRID

Attention behaves as light.

The **Attention Grid** determines what receives active processing.

Attention is finite. When too many demands compete, spotlights flicker and important systems may go dark.

---

# 2.4.16 — DISTRACTION BAZAAR & DOPAMINE ARCADE

The **Distraction Bazaar** contains notifications, rumors, indicators, social media, other traders' opinions, novelty, and bright competing signals.

Inside/near it is the **Dopamine Arcade**, representing overtrading, refreshing P&L, instant-feedback addiction, and impulsive action.

---

# 2.4.17 — PATIENCE GARDENS

The **Patience Gardens** are quiet, minimal, slow, and low-stimulation.

Early Chibi hates them. Advanced Chibi visits voluntarily.

Core lesson:

> **Nothing happening is still a condition.**

Patience is active restraint, not passivity.

---

# 2.4.18 — LOW-POWER DISTRICT

Fatigue appears as the **Low-Power District**.

Lights dim. Systems slow. Discipline weakens. Attention flickers. Threat detection becomes less reliable. CEREBUS connection may degrade.

Sleep is system maintenance: memory sorts, alarms recalibrate, Discipline recharges, and Attention resets.

---

# 2.4.19 — VULNERABILITY MULTIPLIERS

Chibi becomes easier to hijack under:
- sleep deprivation,
- hunger,
- stress,
- drawdown,
- social pressure,
- urgency,
- winning streak,
- isolation.

These are **Vulnerability Multipliers**.

---

# 2.4.20 — SIGNAL WELL

Intuition is represented by the **Signal Well**.

Healthy intuition can be compressed experience. Unhealthy intuition can be emotion pretending to be signal.

Impulse and intuition can feel similar. CEREBUS can help investigate the difference but cannot always classify perfectly.

---

# 2.4.21 — IDENTITY HALL & FOUNDATION

The **Identity Hall** contains reflections such as Trader, Builder, Failure, Winner, Mentor, Fraud, Architect, Explorer.

Identity can stabilize behavior or trap it.

Healthy mature identity:

> **“I am someone who follows process and adapts.”**

Below Identity Hall is **The Foundation**, representing enduring values such as freedom, independence, creation, legacy, helping others, mastery, and curiosity.

Values are foundations. Goals are temporary signs.

---

# 2.4.22 — INNER RESOURCE ECONOMY

Core internal resources:
- Attention,
- Energy,
- Emotional Capacity,
- Discipline,
- Confidence,
- Memory,
- Curiosity.

Different psychological states consume and replenish these resources.

---

# 2.4.23 — CEREBUS ACCESS LIMITS

CEREBUS can detect fatigue patterns, rule deviation, risk changes, behavioral drift, repeated habits, and P&L fixation.

It cannot simply read feelings or delete emotion.

It can provide context, enforce rules, reveal history, trigger locks, and compare perception with measured state.

Emotional regulation remains GLX's responsibility.

---

# 2.4.24 — PERCEPTUAL CONTAMINATION

Inner state can distort Market perception.

Fear makes the Market appear more dangerous.
Greed makes targets look closer.
Ego falsely removes fog.
Fatigue blurs routes.

This is **Perceptual Contamination**.

CEREBUS can compare perceived Market state with measured Market state and issue:

> **PERCEPTION DIVERGENCE**

---

# 2.4.25 — INNER CIVILIZATION

Unnamed background roles may include Memory Clerks, Attention Operators, Discipline Engineers, Risk Wardens, Signal Couriers, Dream Builders, and Archive Keepers.

Named psychological entities belong to Block 3.

---

# 2.4.26 — DAMAGE & LEARNING

Psychological events permanently alter infrastructure.

Examples:
- drawdown makes Threat Tower oversensitive,
- successful discipline creates a better route to the Core,
- repeated FOMO widens the FOMO Tunnel,
- processed loss turns a chamber into an Archive file.

Learning appears as construction: new roads, repaired bridges, closed tunnels, expanded systems.

---

# 2.4.27 — RECOVERY WARD

The **Recovery Ward** supports recovery after burnout, overload, Glitch exposure, failure, or sleep deprivation.

Broken Inner World may have dark Control Room, damaged Discipline Core, random Memory activation, weak Attention, and a quiet Chibi.

Recovery should happen gradually.

---

# 2.4.28 — ASCENDANT INNER WORLD

Ascendant does not remove emotion.

Instead:
- pathways are clear,
- alarms calibrated,
- Discipline stable,
- Strategy clean,
- Golden Quarter smaller,
- Ego Tower no longer dominates,
- Patience Gardens expanded,
- Memory organized,
- Chibi remains expressive but cooperative.

> **Mature operators feel emotion without automatically obeying it.**

---

# 2.4.29 — INNER WORLD ONTOLOGY

CEREBUS-compatible nodes may include emotion, memory, fatigue, rule, behavior, decision, outcome, environment, vulnerability, and confidence.

Possible edges:
- fatigue → lower discipline,
- drawdown → higher threat sensitivity,
- winning streak → ego elevation,
- social pressure → FOMO,
- preparation → confidence.

CEREBUS sees behavioral state. GLX sees Inner World condition.

---

# INNER WORLD NORTH STAR

> **A non-trader recognizes themselves in Chibi's struggle with Fear, Ego, temptation, memory, and self-control, while a trader sees a coherent behavioral model underneath it.**

Central line:

> **The Market does not only test your strategy. It tests the system operating the strategy.**
