## Status
Canonical draft for the long-term mystery, deep-market, uncertainty, time, causal, and research-failure layers of GLX WORLD.

## Purpose
The Hidden Layers represent the boundaries of current understanding.

They exist to create:
- mystery,
- advanced CEREBUS ontology,
- philosophical depth,
- research expeditions,
- historical reconstruction,
- model failure stories,
- long-term lore.

> **The deeper the layer, the less GLX should trust the first explanation.**

# 2.5.1 — KNOWN DEPTHS

## Depth 0 — Outer Reality
Normal life, screens, people, charts.

## Depth 1 — Surface Market
Visible price/session behavior.

## Depth 2 — Structural Market
Liquidity, volatility, positioning, intermarket relationships, regime.

## Depth 3 — Deep Market
Institutional-scale structure, hidden liquidity, temporal relationships, systemic flows.

## Depth 4 — Source Layer
The least understood organization beneath current market representations.

Depth is not a power level. Depth means uncertainty.

Visual:

- sparse architecture,
- black water,
- glowing nodes,
- impossible structures,
- unknown scale.

Compared with Surface Market, there is less clutter but more consequence.

# 2.5.3 — ABYSSAL LIQUIDITY TRENCH

The deepest known part of Liquidity Ocean is:

> **The Abyssal Trench**

At ordinary depth, GLX sees pools and currents. At deep depth, liquidity becomes pressure.

CEREBUS may detect displacement without visible source, absorption, persistent pressure, cross-market effects, or hidden participation.

GLX may perceive enormous shapes beneath black water without ever seeing them clearly.

# 2.5.4 — INSTITUTIONAL LAYER

The Institutional Layer represents the scale of:
- banks,
- asset managers,
- pensions,
- sovereign funds,
- dealers,
- central institutions,
- systematic participants.

Institutions should appear as infrastructure: fleets, factories, rail, vaults, capital engines, and information networks — not omnipotent villains.

# 2.5.5 — MACHINE CITIES

Algorithmic market behavior may appear as:

> **Machine Cities**

Infrastructure runs without visible residents. Routes activate automatically. Orders move continuously. No single operator controls the system.

This communicates automation without conspiracy framing.

# 2.5.6 — MARKET CORE

The existence of **The Market Core** is canonical.

Its nature is not.

Possible beliefs:
- all liquidity eventually connects there,
- it represents emergent price discovery,
- it is a CEREBUS abstraction,
- it does not exist in one place.

Contradictory myths are allowed.

# 2.5.7 — THE SOURCE

The Source is not a being or magical object.

It represents:

> **market causality before humans simplify it into price**

Possible contributors include production, scarcity, expectations, policy, human action, technology, fear, capital, and real-world constraints.

Nobody owns the Source.

# 2.5.8 — CHRONOLAYER

The Time Layer is:

> **The Chronolayer**

Time becomes visible as history, duration, seasonality, cycles, timing windows, and branching possible futures.

This is not time travel. GLX cannot undo trades.

# 2.5.9 — TIME RIVERS

Historical states become **Time Rivers**.

CEREBUS can trace and reconstruct them. GLX can explore prior conditions to understand historical events, cycles, recurring patterns, and regime differences.

# 2.5.10 — POSSIBILITY FOREST

Future states become **The Possibility Forest**.

Paths branch repeatedly. Each path has conditions, probability, consequence, and invalidation.

Without constraints, the search space becomes impossible.

# 2.5.11 — PRUNING LAW

GLX narrows possibilities using:
- evidence,

CEREBUS helps prune low-relevance branches. GLX still exercises judgment.

# 2.5.12 — GLITCH CORRIDORS

Glitch Corridors are unstable pathways that may connect:
- Outer ↔ Market,
- Market ↔ Inner,
- Present ↔ historical reconstruction,
- CEREBUS ↔ unknown structures.

They can appear, vanish, loop, split, or expose impossible architecture.

The Black Door may connect to one.

Status:
> possible, not confirmed.

# 2.5.13 — UNMAPPED DARK

Beyond reliable CEREBUS coverage is:

> **The Unmapped Dark**

It means:

> **we do not know**

Unknown does not automatically mean hidden by someone. Some things are unknown because data is incomplete, causality is complex, regimes changed, or no reliable model exists.

---

# 2.5.14 — CAUSAL WEB

At deep level CEREBUS may visualize **The Causal Web** linking economic events, policy, flows, positioning, behavior, price, and feedback.

Correlation and causality must look different.

Correlation = bridge.
Causal pathway = directed mechanism/flow.

---

# 2.5.15 — REFLEXIVITY ZONES

Feedback loops may create **Reflexivity Zones**.

Example:
falling price → fear → selling → lower price → more fear.

These support stories about bubbles, squeezes, panic, mania, and self-reinforcing behavior.

---

# 2.5.16 — SIGNAL GRAVEYARD

A deep region for obsolete or failed edges:

> **The Signal Graveyard**

Contains broken antennas, dead lights, retired tools, old maps, and rusted systems.

Signals may die because of regime change, adaptation, structural change, costs, overuse, false discovery, or random luck.

Dead edges remain valuable as lessons.

---

# 2.5.17 — OVERFIT MAZE

Canonical research-hazard location:

> **The Overfit Maze**

Inside, everything works perfectly. Historical paths align and tests look beautiful. Outside, the model fails.

Entrance warning:

> **IN-SAMPLE TERRITORY**

The Maze visualizes overfitting and false confidence.

---

# 2.5.18 — NOISE SEA

The Noise Sea represents:

> **information without useful signal**

It contains constant movement, lights, waves, voices, and false structure.

GLX can become overwhelmed without learning anything.

---

# 2.5.19 — SIGNAL ISLANDS & MIRAGES

Inside the Noise Sea are candidate **Signal Islands**.

Some are real. Some are Mirages representing false positives, spurious correlations, random pattern, and unsupported interpretation.

CEREBUS must test them.

---

# 2.5.20 — DATA CAVERNS

Raw historical information lives in **The Data Caverns**.

They may contain missing records, duplicates, broken timestamps, biased datasets, different eras, and corrupted sections.

Data quality becomes visible infrastructure.

---

# 2.5.21 — OBSERVATION PRESSURE & EDGE DECAY

The world adapts to being studied.

A once-hidden route may become crowded after discovery. This is **Observation Pressure**.

When too many participants use a route, traffic increases, speed falls, reward shrinks, and congestion rises.

Edge physically decays.

---

# 2.5.22 — ADAPTIVE MARKET LAW

> **The world learns back.**

GLX studies the world. Other participants adapt. The Atlas can never be finished.

# 2.5.23 — NULL ZONE

> **The Null Zone**

CEREBUS may conclude:

> **No actionable structure.**

This is not failure. It represents a rejected hypothesis and scientific maturity.
