# 1 — BLOCK 2 ACHIEVEMENT

Block 2 transforms GLX WORLD from a character-centered brand into a coherent layered universe.

We now have four major reality systems:

## Outer World
Grounded human reality and the GLX Quant Lab.

## Market World
Financial structure rendered as geography, ecology, weather, civilization, and movement.

## Inner World
Psychology rendered as internal civilization and behavioral infrastructure.

## Hidden Layers
The limits of knowledge, model failure, deep causality, time, uncertainty, and long-term mystery.

These systems are linked rather than isolated.

---

# 2 — CORE WORLD MODEL

The conceptual chain is:

> **Reality → Structure → Interpretation → Decision → Consequence → Memory → Adaptation**

CEREBUS sees:
- entities,
- states,
- graphs,
- edges,
- probabilities,
- regimes,
- transitions,
- constraints.

GLX sees:
- people,
- places,
- weather,
- roads,
- creatures,
- tools,
- conflicts,
- environments.

The audience sees:
- story.

---

# 3 — MASS-AUDIENCE STANDARD

Every major world concept should pass both tests:

> **Would this still be compelling if the viewer knew nothing about markets?**

and:

> **Would an experienced market participant recognize meaningful logic underneath it?**

The project should not choose between entertainment and intelligence when both can coexist.

---

# 4 — WORLD CONSISTENCY LAWS

## Law 1 — Nothing Is Metaphorical by Accident
Recurring fantasy elements require meaning.

## Law 2 — The Market Is Not Evil
It is beautiful, dangerous, alive, and indifferent.

## Law 3 — Bull and Bear Are Forces, Not Moral Alignments
Neither side is inherently heroic.

## Law 4 — Edge Is Navigation, Not Omnipotence
Better understanding improves movement; it does not control reality.

## Law 5 — Risk Is the Price of Movement
Opportunity without exposure does not exist.

## Law 6 — Information Is Light
Reliable knowledge illuminates.

## Law 7 — Uncertainty Is Fog
Fog can be reduced, never eliminated.

## Law 8 — Regimes Change the World
A state change should alter environment, tool usefulness, routes, behavior, and risk.

## Law 9 — The World Learns Back
Participants adapt. Edge decays. Maps become obsolete.

## Law 10 — The Atlas Is Permanently Unfinished
No final market map exists.

---

# 5 — CEREBUS WORLD ROLE

CEREBUS must remain:
- mapmaker,
- telescope,
- filter,
- translator,
- state estimator,
- research assistant,
- behavioral checker.

CEREBUS must not become:
- oracle,
- infallible prophet,
- magical prediction engine,
- secret ruler of the Market.

Core relationship:

> **CEREBUS expands perception. GLX provides judgment.**

# 6 — GLX WORLD DEPTH STACK

Every important concept can eventually support:

## Story Layer
What happened?

## Intuition Layer
What does it feel like / resemble?

## Trader Layer
What market mechanic is represented?

## Lab Layer
How is it measured or tested?

Future Blocks should preserve this depth stack.

# 7 — WORLD AS EDUCATIONAL ENGINE

Preferred educational sequence:

1. viewer sees compelling event,
2. viewer asks what it means,
3. story reveals intuitive logic,
4. GLX explains mechanic,
5. CEREBUS reveals deeper structure if appropriate.

Avoid leading every concept with textbook exposition.

Curiosity should pull the viewer deeper.

# 11 — BLOCK 3 HANDOFF RULE

Block 3 must populate the world without shrinking it.

For every proposed entity ask:

1. What real force, participant, behavior, or idea does it represent?
2. Where does it belong in established geography?
3. Is it an Actor, Force, Institution, Archetype, Species, or Psychological Entity?
4. What does it want?
5. What can it influence?
6. What can it not control?
7. How does GLX encounter it?
8. Can a non-trader understand the character?
9. Can an expert understand the deeper meaning?
10. Does it create reusable story tension?

# 13 — POWER-SCALING GUIDELINE

Do not use ordinary fantasy power scaling.

Power in GLX WORLD comes from:
- information,
- access,
- capital,
- timing,
- risk capacity,
- coordination,
- adaptability,
- structural influence,
- knowledge.

A participant can have enormous capital and poor understanding. Another can have little capital and excellent information.

This creates smarter conflict.

---

# 14 — ENTITY MORALITY GUIDELINE

Avoid simplistic:
- Bull = good,
- Bear = evil,
- institution = villain,
- retail = victim,
- CEREBUS = savior,
- Fear = enemy.

Most forces should have healthy expression, destructive expression, and context-dependent function.

---

# 15 — PERSISTENCE GUIDELINE

World consequences should persist.

Examples:
- damaged bridge remains damaged,
- repaired Discipline pathway remains improved,
- retired edge remains in Signal Graveyard,
- historical scar remains on map,
- old Lab damage remains visible,
- relationships can weaken over time.

Continuity creates reality.

---

# 16 — VISUAL GUIDELINE

When introducing a new location or entity, prefer one iconic visual idea over ten generic fantasy details.

Examples:
- London = fog + iron + bells + bridges,
- Asia = moonlit boundaries + lantern liquidity,
- New York = capital grids + vertical scale,
- Fear = Threat Tower,
- Greed = Projection Palace,
- uncertainty = fog,
- information = light.

Recognizability beats clutter.

---

# 17 — FINAL BLOCK 2 FREEZE TEST

Block 2 is ready to freeze if future work respects these principles:

> The world is coherent enough to tell stories without explaining every market term.

> The world is deep enough that experts can find real structure beneath fantasy.

> CEREBUS ontology and GLX visual language describe the same underlying system.

> Psychology is treated as a system, not a joke.

> Mystery preserves uncertainty instead of replacing it with magic.

> No force grants permanent certainty or control.

> The world can expand without breaking its foundational laws.

---

# BLOCK 2 FINAL STATEMENT

Block 2 establishes a world where:

> **markets become geography, psychology becomes civilization, research becomes exploration, data becomes light, uncertainty becomes fog, and CEREBUS becomes the mapmaker helping GLX navigate a world that can be understood—but never completely finished.**

Block 3 may now begin.
