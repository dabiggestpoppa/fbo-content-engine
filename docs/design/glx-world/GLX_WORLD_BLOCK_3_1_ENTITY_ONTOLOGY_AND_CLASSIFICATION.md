# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 3.1: ENTITY ONTOLOGY & CLASSIFICATION

## Status
Canonical draft for the classification, agency, influence, persistence, manifestation, relationship, discovery, and power rules governing recurring entities in GLX WORLD.

## Purpose
Block 3 populates the world created in Block 2. Before individual characters are designed, GLX WORLD needs a controlled ontology defining what kinds of beings may exist, what they represent, how they interact, and what they can or cannot control.

> **Characters make the world emotionally legible. Ontology keeps the characters intellectually honest.**

## Core Entity Law
Every recurring entity must represent something real enough to explain: a market force, participant, psychological system, institution, information structure, behavioral pattern, economic mechanism, or narrative relationship.

> **Every entity has an ontology underneath the design.**

## Entity ≠ Force
Not everything should become a person. Liquidity, Volatility, Risk, Time, Capital, Information, and Uncertainty are primarily forces. They may create environmental behavior, manifestations, or fauna without becoming talking mascots.

## Six Primary Entity Classes

### Class I — Archetypes
Ancient recurring market behaviors such as Bull and Bear. They recur across eras, markets, and asset classes and represent directional behavior itself.

### Class II — Forces
Non-sentient or semi-sentient systems such as Liquidity, Volatility, Risk, Time, Capital, Information, and Uncertainty.

### Class III — Psychological Entities
Internal systems such as Fear, Greed, Ego, Doubt, Curiosity, and Discipline.

### Class IV — Institutional Actors
Central banks, dealers, asset managers, funds, banks, sovereign capital, and systematic participants. Institutions are systems and groups, not omnipotent villains.

### Class V — Natives / Species
Market World fauna such as Pip Birds, Stopfish, Spread Eels, Volatility Rays, and Leverage Leeches.

### Class VI — Unique Entities
GLX, CEREBUS, future rivals, mentors, explorers, supporting cast, and unknown Deep Market entities.

## Agency Scale
- **A0 — Environmental:** no conscious action.
- **A1 — Reactive:** automatic response.
- **A2 — Instinctive:** simple goals.
- **A3 — Strategic:** choice, adaptation, cooperation, deception.
- **A4 — Self-Aware:** understands itself and others.
- **A5 — Emergent / Unknown:** behavior appears intelligent but true agency is unresolved.

## Influence ≠ Control
Influence changes probabilities and conditions. Control guarantees outcome. Very few things in GLX WORLD possess true control.

## Influence Scale
- **I0 — Local**
- **I1 — District**
- **I2 — Realm**
- **I3 — Cross-Realm**
- **I4 — Systemic**
- **I5 — Foundational**

Foundational does not mean “strongest fighter.” It means part of the rules of reality.

## Manifestation vs True Form
Many entities have regional manifestations rather than one fixed appearance. The same Bull archetype can appear restrained in Asia, scarred and forceful in London, capital-armored in New York, or digital in Crypto while preserving the same underlying meaning.

## Persistence
- **Temporary:** event-bound.
- **Recurring:** returns repeatedly.
- **Persistent:** always exists somewhere.
- **Structural:** cannot be removed without changing reality.

GLX cannot permanently destroy structural forces.

## Defeat Law
Victory depends on ontology. Fear is regulated, not destroyed. Bear is survived, understood, or traded. A bad habit can be weakened or closed. A dead strategy can be retired. No foundational force can be “defeated” in a way that breaks world logic.

## Relationship Types
- Adversarial
- Competitive
- Symbiotic
- Dependent
- Predatory
- Regulatory
- Informational
- Reflexive

Fear ≠ Risk. Greed ≠ Capital. Bull ≠ Liquidity. Their relationships create the story.

## Entity Needs
- Bull → continuation / upward expansion
- Bear → downward expansion
- Fear → safety
- Greed → greater potential reward
- Ego → identity stability
- Doubt → error prevention
- Curiosity → understanding
- Discipline → behavioral consistency
- CEREBUS → reduce uncertainty / map structure

## Healthy vs Distorted Expression
Psychological entities require functional, elevated, and distorted states. Fear may progress from Awareness to Concern to Panic. Greed from Ambition to Expansion to Risk Blindness. Ego from Confidence to Certainty to Evidence Rejection.

## Entity Energy Sources
Visual strength must come from real conditions. Bull may strengthen from sustained buying, breadth, capital flow, and continuation. Bear from selling, deleveraging, negative breadth, and breakdown. Fear from uncertainty, drawdown, and negative memory. Greed from profit, social proof, and winning streaks.

> **No random power-ups.**

## Asymmetric Power
Different entities dominate different dimensions. Institutions may have huge Capital and limited agility. GLX may have lower Capital but higher adaptability. CEREBUS may have extreme processing and limited judgment. Chibi has extreme emotional influence. There is no universal power level.

## Visibility Levels
- **V0 — Hidden:** only effects observable.
- **V1 — Trace:** footprints/signals.
- **V2 — Partial:** silhouette/shadow.
- **V3 — Manifested:** clearly visible.
- **V4 — Named / Understood:** GLX has classification.
- **V5 — Integrated Knowledge:** behavior reasonably understood.

## Entity Discovery Protocol
OBSERVED → ANOMALY → TRACKED → HYPOTHESIS → CLASSIFIED → VALIDATED.

This mirrors GLX research methodology.

## Naming Law
Use clear names first: Fear, Greed, Bull, Bear, CEREBUS, Pip Birds. Poetic titles should be earned by deeper lore. Over-naming raises the entry barrier.

## Silhouette Law
Major entities need recognizable silhouettes. Bull = horns + massive forward posture. Bear = large low heavy shape. Fear = narrow/protective. Greed = expansive/open. CEREBUS = three-node motif.

## Color Ownership
GLX owns hot pink + black + gold. Other entities should not casually duplicate the full combination. Early directions: Bull warm gold/green/black; Bear deep red/dark brown/black; Fear cold red/warning white/shadow; Greed gold/emerald; CEREBUS electric blue/purple with controlled pink.

## Audio Language
Major entities should eventually own arrival sounds, environmental cues, movement language, and dialogue treatment if applicable.

## Entrance / Exit Law
Major entities should change the environment before fully appearing, and their exit should communicate whether the state ended, weakened, or moved elsewhere.

## Native-Layer Law
Every entity gets a native layer. Bull/Bear are Market-native. Fear/Greed are Inner-native. GLX crosses all layers. CEREBUS spans Outer + Market interpretation + limited Inner interface. Cross-layer capability should remain rare and meaningful.

## Entity Death
Structural forces cannot die. Archetypes cannot permanently die. Psychological entities cannot permanently die while the human remains capable of that emotion. Market fauna may die. Strategies may die conceptually. Unique mortal characters may die if story eventually requires it. CEREBUS mortality remains unresolved.

## Entity Evolution
Recurring entities evolve through relationship, accumulated history, regime, deeper understanding, and world change. Early GLX may see Bear as a monster; later he understands Bear; eventually he can ride alongside Bear during a short trade.

## From Enemy to Force
A central progression:

Early: **Bull good. Bear bad.**

Later: **Both are opportunities.**

Advanced: **They are conditions.**

## Misclassification
GLX and CEREBUS can classify incorrectly. Wrong classification has consequences. Reclassification is not retcon: the earlier misunderstanding can remain canonically wrong.

Possible knowledge states:
- KNOWN
- PARTIALLY KNOWN
- CONTESTED
- UNKNOWN
- MISCLASSIFIED

## Mass-Audience Character Test
Every major entity must pass both:

> **Would I care about this character if I knew nothing about trading?**

and:

> **Does its deeper financial or psychological meaning remain coherent to someone who does understand markets?**

## Merch / Icon Test
Recurring entities should translate into stickers, icons, silhouettes, shirts, posters, animation, and emoji — but merch follows canon, not the reverse.

Each major entity should eventually receive:

- Influence

- Core Function

## Morality Law
No character may violate the world philosophy merely because the story needs a villain. Avoid evil Bear, evil institution, evil Fear, or evil Volatility. Prefer conflicting incentives, dangerous conditions, information asymmetry, competition, and poor adaptation.
