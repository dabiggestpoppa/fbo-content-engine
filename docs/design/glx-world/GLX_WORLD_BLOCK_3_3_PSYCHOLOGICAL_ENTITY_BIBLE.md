# GLX WORLD — BLOCK 3.3: PSYCHOLOGICAL ENTITY BIBLE

## Status
Canonical draft for Fear, Greed, Ego, Doubt, Curiosity, Discipline, and their relationship to Chibi, GLX, CEREBUS, and Inner World infrastructure.

## Purpose
The Inner World becomes a true recurring civilization only when its psychological systems gain recognizable agency without becoming simplistic “good” and “evil” emotions.

> **None of these voices should disappear. The goal is to know when each deserves influence.**

Chibi remains the emotional interface through which GLX experiences their pressure.

---

# 3.3.1 — CORE RULE

Every psychological entity must possess:
- a legitimate function,
- a useful state,
- an elevated state,
- a distorted state,
- a visual language,
- an influence pattern,
- a relationship with Chibi,
- a relationship with GLX,
- a CEREBUS-observable footprint,
- explicit limitations.

No “evil emotion” writing.

---

# 3.3.2 — FEAR

Core function:

> **Protection from perceived danger**

Fear is not trying to ruin GLX.

Fear is trying to keep him safe.

Problems emerge when:

> **perceived danger ≠ actual danger**

### Ontology

**Class:** Psychological Entity  
**Native Layer:** Inner World  
**Primary Territory:** Fear District  
**Agency:** A3  
**Influence:** I0–I2 normally; higher during crisis  
**Persistence:** Persistent  
**Core Need:** Safety

### Visual Design

Fear should look like a paranoid guardian rather than a demon:
- tall, narrow silhouette,
- layered protective clothing,
- warning lights embedded in armor,
- sensor motifs,
- protective cape/barrier,
- partially obscured face,
- red/white warning accents.

Healthy Fear watches.

Distorted Fear barricades.

### Escalation

F0 — Resting  
F1 — Alert  
F2 — Concern  
F3 — Defensive  
F4 — Panic  
F5 — Lockdown

At F5, Fear may lock the Operator Chair itself.

### Voice

Healthy:
> “Check that.”

> “Risk is expanding.”

Distorted:
> “Get out.”

> “Now.”

### Relationship to Chibi

Fear activates Chibi's emergency behavior:
- sweating,
- lever pulling,
- P&L fixation,
- catastrophe projection.

Chibi can also reveal Fear before Outer GLX admits it.

### Relationship to Memory

Fear heavily consults negative memory.

It may physically carry an old file:

> “Remember this?”

Sometimes useful.

Sometimes out of context.

### Relationship to CEREBUS

CEREBUS calibrates Fear against evidence.

Fear:
> “THIS IS EXACTLY LIKE LAST TIME.”

CEREBUS:
> “Similarity: 31%.”

Or sometimes:

> “Similarity: 87%. Risk state elevated.”

Fear can be right.

### Cannot Do

Fear cannot:
- know the future,
- measure objective risk perfectly,
- independently execute,
- permanently distinguish intuition from anxiety.

Fear is a detector, not an oracle.

---

# 3.3.3 — GREED

Core function:

> **Expansion toward potential reward**

Healthy Greed is close to ambition.

Without expansion pressure, GLX would never:
- grow,
- build,
- take calculated risk,
- pursue opportunity.

Distorted Greed turns:

> **possibility into entitlement**

### Ontology

**Class:** Psychological Entity  
**Native Layer:** Inner World  
**Primary Territory:** Golden Quarter  
**Agency:** A3  
**Influence:** I0–I2  
**Persistence:** Persistent  
**Core Need:** Expansion / reward

### Visual Design

Greed should be charismatic:
- elegant,
- attractive,
- polished,
- gold/emerald accents,
- open posture,
- surrounded by projected possibilities.

Greed should look like:

> **the best salesman in your own mind**

### Voice

Greed persuades rather than forces.

> “Just one more.”

> “Look how close it is.”

> “You've been right all week.”

> “Size it a little bigger.”

### Escalation

G0 — Content  
G1 — Interested  
G2 — Excited  
G3 — Expanding  
G4 — Overreach  
G5 — Risk Blindness

At G5, Risk displays disappear behind gold projections.

### Relationship to Chibi

Chibi loves Greed.

Greed:
> “Imagine if we double size.”

Chibi:
already wearing yacht-captain gear.

### Fear / Greed Conflict

Fear:
> “Close.”

Greed:
> “Add.”

Chibi:
spinning between them.

This is the Swing Corridor embodied.

### Ego / Greed Coalition

Winning streak:

Ego:
> “We're seeing this better than everyone.”

Greed:
> “Exactly. So size it.”

This becomes the Overconfidence Coalition.

### Cannot Do

Greed cannot:
- create edge,
- increase probability,
- create liquidity,
- guarantee reward,
- convert unrealized P&L into realized outcome.

Its power is persuasion.

---

# 3.3.4 — EGO

Core function:

> **Protect identity and maintain coherent self-belief**

Healthy Ego lets GLX:
- recover from loss,
- trust his work,
- speak confidently,
- keep building,
- resist external noise.

### Ontology

**Class:** Psychological Entity  
**Native Layer:** Inner World  
**Primary Territory:** Ego Tower / Confirmation Hall  
**Agency:** A3–A4-like presentation  
**Influence:** I0–I3 internally  
**Persistence:** Persistent  
**Core Need:** Self-coherence / status protection

### Visual Design

Ego should resemble GLX slightly:
- taller,
- cleaner,
- impossibly polished,
- immaculate glasses,
- pristine clothing,
- mirror motifs,
- subtle gold.

Ego looks like:

> **the version of GLX who believes he has nothing left to learn**

### Voice

Smooth.

Certain.

Rarely loud.

> “We already know this.”

> “You don't need another test.”

> “They're wrong.”

> “Trust yourself.”

The words may be healthy or dangerous depending on context.

### Distortion

As Ego strengthens:
- mirrors grow,
- Confirmation Hall expands,
- contradictions disappear,
- fog falsely thins,
- GLX's image appears everywhere.

The environment increasingly agrees with him.

### Public-Audience Pressure

Followers, students, praise, public predictions, and winning streaks can strengthen Ego Tower.

Creator success itself becomes psychological story material.

### Relationship to CEREBUS

CEREBUS:
> “Claim unsupported by current evidence.”

Ego:
> “Nobody asked you.”

CEREBUS remains calm.

### Cannot Do

Ego cannot:
- change data,
- alter history,
- create edge,
- permanently eliminate contradiction.

But it can hide contradiction from perception.

---

# 3.3.5 — DOUBT

Core function:

> **Prevent error by challenging certainty**

Without Doubt, GLX accepts first explanations too easily.

### Ontology

**Class:** Psychological Entity  
**Native Layer:** Inner World  
**Primary Territory:** The Crossroads  
**Agency:** A3  
**Influence:** I0–I2  
**Persistence:** Persistent  
**Core Need:** Error prevention

### Visual Design

Doubt should feel analytical rather than timid:
- overlapping silhouettes,
- split lens,
- branching maps,
- neutral gray/blue,
- many possible roads.

### Voice

Questions.

> “What if we're wrong?”

> “What's the other explanation?”

> “Did we test that?”

> “What changes if this assumption fails?”

Distorted Doubt repeats:

> “But what if...”

without end.

### Infinite Crossroads

Maximum Doubt creates:

> **Infinite Crossroads**

Every route branches again.

GLX cannot move.

CEREBUS:
> “Decision window closing.”

### Healthy Alliances

Curiosity + Doubt:
> Research.

Fear + Doubt:
> potential paralysis.

### Cannot Do

Doubt cannot:
- prove something false merely by questioning it,
- eliminate uncertainty,
- decide when enough evidence is enough.

Judgment must eventually decide.

---

# 3.3.6 — CURIOSITY

Core function:

> **Seek understanding**

Curiosity is one of the central engines of GLX's personality.

### Ontology

**Class:** Psychological Entity  
**Native Layer:** Inner World with strong cross-layer influence  
**Primary Territory:** Imagination District / Idea Foundry / Signal Well  
**Agency:** A3  
**Influence:** I0–I3  
**Persistence:** Persistent  
**Core Need:** Discovery

### Visual Design

Curiosity should feel:
- quick,
- alert,
- sharp,
- investigative,
- exploratory,
- electric blue/violet accented,
- tool- and notebook-oriented.

Not childlike.

Chibi already owns exaggerated childlike emotionality.

### Voice

> “What's that?”

> “Why?”

> “That doesn't fit.”

> “Can we test it?”

> “What happens if...”

### Distorted Curiosity

Curiosity can become:

> **Obsession**

It may:
- ignore sleep,
- chase irrelevant anomalies,
- continue after diminishing returns,
- open Restricted doors,
- mistake novelty for importance.

### Relationship to Chibi

Chibi Detective + Curiosity can become a chaotic research pair.

### Relationship to CEREBUS

Curiosity generates questions.

CEREBUS evaluates.

Doubt challenges.

GLX judges.

This forms the Research Coalition.

### Cannot Do

Curiosity cannot:
- decide importance,
- decide usefulness,
- know when to stop,
- guarantee discovery.

# 3.3.7 — DISCIPLINE

Discipline is both:

> **infrastructure**

and

> **a system avatar**

The Discipline Core remains the infrastructure.

The avatar through which the system becomes socially legible is provisionally:

> **The Warden**

The public name may remain simply Discipline until earned.

### Ontology

**Class:** Psychological Entity + System Avatar  
**Native Layer:** Inner World  
**Primary Territory:** Discipline Core  
**Agency:** A3  
**Influence:** I0–I3  
**Persistence:** Structural-psychological  
**Core Need:** Behavioral consistency

### Visual Design

Discipline is:
- calm,
- minimal,
- precise,
- darkly armored,
- lightly marked with white/gold/blue indicators,
- associated with keys, locks, permissions, and process.

Not a drill sergeant.

Not a motivational coach.

It looks like:

> **the person quietly keeping the building from burning down**

### Voice

Short.

> “Rule says wait.”

> “Size exceeds limit.”

> “No confirmation.”

> “Target reached.”

> “We're done.”

### Philosophy

Discipline is not punishment.

Its purpose is:

> **preserve future optionality**

It protects:
- capital,
- attention,
- confidence,
- time,
- ability to act tomorrow.

### Relationship to Chibi

Chibi:
trying to pry open a locked BUY control.

Discipline:
standing silently behind him.

Chibi slowly stops.

### Failure Modes

Discipline can fail because:
- rules are wrong,
- rules are too rigid,
- fatigue causes brownout,
- environment changes,
- exception logic is poor.

Rigid discipline:
> “Always do X.”

Adaptive discipline:
> “Follow the valid process for the current condition.”

### Relationship to CEREBUS

CEREBUS can:
- monitor,
- audit,
- enforce approved constraints,
- flag degraded rules.

CEREBUS should not silently rewrite Discipline rules.

### Cannot Do

Discipline cannot:
- create strategy quality,
- make bad rules good,
- eliminate emotion,
- guarantee profit.

It preserves chosen behavior.

# 3.3.8 — THE THREE TENSIONS

The six psychological entities naturally form three paired tensions.

## Survival Tension

> **Fear ↔ Greed**

Protection vs expansion.

## Belief Tension

> **Ego ↔ Doubt**

Confidence vs error checking.

## Growth Tension

> **Curiosity ↔ Discipline**

Exploration vs constraint.

Healthy performance does not eliminate these tensions.

It keeps them productive.

# 3.3.9 — BALANCE LAW

Fear without Greed:
never moves.

Greed without Fear:
destroys risk.

Ego without Doubt:
becomes delusion.

Doubt without confidence:
never acts.

Curiosity without Discipline:
obsesses.

Discipline without Curiosity:
becomes rigid.

> **Maturity is coordination, not maximum suppression.**

Chibi is not a seventh psychological entity.

Chibi is:

> **the emotional interface through which GLX experiences their pressure**

Fear alarms Chibi.

Greed persuades Chibi.

Ego flatters Chibi.

Doubt confuses Chibi.

Curiosity excites Chibi.

Discipline restrains Chibi.

# 3.3.11 — CEREBUS' POSITION

CEREBUS sits partially outside the emotional system.

It observes footprints.

It does not fully experience the state.

CEREBUS:
> “Fear state elevated.”

Chibi:
> “YOU THINK?”

This contrast must remain.

roads multiply.

> **PERCEPTION DIVERGENCE**
