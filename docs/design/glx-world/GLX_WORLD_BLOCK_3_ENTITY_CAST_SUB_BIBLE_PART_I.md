Canonical sub-bible, Part I of II.

# EC.2 — CORE TRIANGLE

> **CEREBUS = Logic**

> **GLX = Judgment**

> **Chibi = Emotion**

CEREBUS asks:
> “What does the evidence say?”

Chibi asks:
> “How does this feel?”

GLX asks:
> “What am I going to do?”

This triangle informs dialogue, decisions, comedy, conflict, education, and resolution.

---

# EC.3 — GLX ROLE

GLX is not the strongest being in the universe.

He is:

> **the central interpreter**

His role is to move between Outer World, Market World, Inner World, CEREBUS representation, and human judgment.

GLX translates systems into choices.

---

# EC.4 — CHIBI ROLE

Chibi is not a separate clone or simple sidekick.

He is:

> **the visible emotional operating system of GLX**

He makes emotion readable, funny, relatable, and reusable while still being capable of growth.

---

# EC.5 — CEREBUS ROLE

CEREBUS is the scale intelligence.

It processes, compares, remembers, and classifies beyond normal human bandwidth.

Its role is:

> **expand what can be seen without erasing uncertainty**

---

# EC.6 — BULL / BEAR ROLE

Bull and Bear are persistent directional archetypes rather than ordinary personalities.

Their story functions:
- make direction legible,
- create recognizable Market forces,
- support trend/chop/regime stories,
- externalize directional conflict,
- visually track GLX maturity.

Relationship arc:
Fear → Fight → Study → Alignment → Dual Competence → Respect.

---

# EC.7 — PSYCHOLOGICAL CAST STRUCTURE

## Survival Tension
Fear ↔ Greed

## Belief Tension
Ego ↔ Doubt

## Growth Tension
Curiosity ↔ Discipline

These tensions are systems to balance, not problems to eliminate.

---

# EC.8 — FEAR

Narrative function:
- threat detection,
- caution,
- panic,
- avoidance,
- protective overreach.

Healthy:
specific caution.

Distorted:
globalized threat.

Visual identity:
warning guardian.

Key relationship:
Fear + Memory.

---

# EC.9 — GREED

Narrative function:
- ambition,
- expansion,
- reward projection,
- overreach,
- risk blindness.

Healthy:
growth pressure.

Distorted:
entitlement to imagined reward.

Visual:
charismatic golden persuader.

Key relationship:
Greed + Ego.

---

# EC.10 — EGO

Narrative function:
- confidence,
- identity,
- public-image pressure,
- confirmation bias,
- refusal to update.

Healthy:
stable self-belief.

Distorted:
evidence rejection.

Visual:
idealized GLX-like mirror figure.

Key relationship:
Ego + audience.

---

# EC.11 — DOUBT

Narrative function:
- skepticism,
- error prevention,
- alternative hypotheses,
- paralysis.

Healthy:
checks certainty.

Distorted:
never allows closure.

Visual:
branching-map analyst.

Key relationship:
Doubt + Curiosity.

---

# EC.12 — CURIOSITY

Narrative function:
- discovery,
- investigation,
- experimentation,
- obsession,
- mystery entry.

Healthy:
learning.

Distorted:
compulsive investigation.

Visual:
sharp explorer/researcher.

Key relationship:
Curiosity + CEREBUS.

---

# EC.13 — DISCIPLINE

Narrative function:
- boundary,
- process,
- restraint,
- consistency,
- precommitment.

Healthy:
adaptive rule-following.

Distorted:
rigidity.

Visual:
calm system Warden.

Key relationship:
Discipline + CEREBUS.

---

# EC.14 — PSYCHOLOGICAL CAST RULE

No psychological entity becomes the villain forever.

Fear may save GLX.
Greed may supply ambition.
Ego may preserve confidence.
Doubt may prevent catastrophe.
Curiosity may discover edge.
Discipline may become too rigid.

Their value is conditional.

---

# EC.15 — THE COUNCIL

The six psychological entities may occasionally converge as **The Council**.

The Council represents competing internal advice.

Everyone may speak.

Nobody decides for GLX.

Potential sequence:

Fear:
> “Too dangerous.”

Greed:
> “Too good to pass.”

Ego:
> “We know this.”

Doubt:
> “Do we?”

Curiosity:
> “Let's test one thing.”

Discipline:
> “Decision window closes in two minutes.”

Everyone turns to GLX.

Judgment decides.

# EC.17 — ROOKIE EXPLORER

Purpose:

> **audience gateway**

Traits:
- intelligent,
- inexperienced,
- curious,
- emotionally readable,
- willing to ask obvious questions.

Critical rule:

> **inexperienced ≠ stupid**

---

# EC.18 — GROUNDED FRIEND

Purpose:

> **protect GLX's humanity**

This character knows GLX outside the Market, challenges obsession, and reminds him that ordinary life exists.

---

# EC.19 — MACRO CARTOGRAPHER

Purpose:

> **expand scale**

Represents policy, rates, global flows, geopolitics, history, and long-horizon context.

Must have blind spots. No omniscient elder.

---

# EC.20 — SYSTEMS ENGINEER

Purpose:

> **challenge imagination with implementation**

This role forces architecture, reliability, security, constraints, and real build limits.

It helps prevent CEREBUS from becoming magic.

---

# EC.21 — RISK WARDEN

Purpose:

> **capital survival perspective**

Represents risk budgets, exposure, leverage, drawdown, and portfolio survival.

A core conflict question:

> **How much consequence does one idea deserve?**

---

# EC.22 — RIVAL OPERATOR

Purpose:

> **philosophical competition**

The rival must be competent, have independent goals, win sometimes, and expose real GLX blind spots.

Possible philosophies:
- pure quant,
- pure discretionary,
- high-leverage,
- macro-first,
- speed-first.

The rival must never exist only to lose.

---

# EC.23 — RIVAL / MENTOR LAWS

> **The rival must be right sometimes.**

> **The mentor must be wrong sometimes.**

Otherwise the cast becomes ego fantasy instead of a believable learning system.

---

# EC.24 — SUPPORTING CAST INDEPENDENCE

Supporting characters need goals outside GLX.

They may disagree, leave, return, evolve, and outperform GLX in specific domains.

Nobody exists solely to praise him.

---

# EC.25 — INSTITUTIONAL CAST

Institutions generally function as collective actors:
- Dealer Houses
- Asset Houses
- Sovereign Houses
- Policy Towers
- Systematic Houses
- Hedge Houses
- Commercial Houses

Their categories should be structurally legible before individual leaders appear.

---

# EC.26 — DEALER HOUSE ROLE

Story function:
- liquidity,
- routing,
- inventory,
- client flow,
- stress transmission.

Visual:
ports, switching yards, warehouses, bridges.

Dealer stories are about infrastructure and constraint—not secret control.

---

# EC.27 — ASSET / SOVEREIGN / POLICY / SYSTEMATIC / HEDGE / COMMERCIAL ROLES

## Asset Houses
Long-duration capital, mandates, benchmarks, huge mass, limited agility.

## Sovereign Houses
Reserve/state-linked capital and non-profit-maximizing objectives.

## Policy Towers
Rates, communication, facilities, and large but conditional environmental influence.

## Systematic Houses
Algorithms, rule-driven execution, speed and consistency, but regime/shared-model risk.

## Hedge Houses
Adaptive tactical capital and strong future-rival territory.

## Commercial Houses
Real-economy hedging and non-speculative participation.

---

# EC.28 — RETAIL CARAVANS / SOCIAL HERDS

Retail Caravans represent decentralized independent participation.

Strengths:
agility, niche focus, ability not to act.

Weaknesses:
limited capital, uneven information, emotional contagion.

Herding is not retail-only. Professionals and institutions herd too.

---

# EC.29 — INFORMATION BROKERS / HERALDS

Information Brokers collect/analyze.

Heralds transmit publicly.

Neither guarantees truth.

Story tension can come from accurate information + bad interpretation or bad information + strong confidence.

---

# EC.30 — MARKET CARTOGRAPHERS

Market Cartographers map regimes, relationships, historical states, and flows.

GLX is one.

CEREBUS is his technical partner.

Other Cartographers can become the intellectual culture of GLX WORLD.

---

# EC.31 — TRUE VILLAIN RULE

Villainy comes from choices, not categories.

Valid villainous behaviors:
- intentional deception,
- exploitation,
- hidden risk,
- fraud,
- corruption,
- reckless power,
- manipulation of vulnerable people.

Invalid shortcut:
> “large institution = evil.”

---

# EC.32 — FALSE PROPHET / EXTRACTOR

## False Prophet
Represents certainty theater, selective wins, hidden losses, absolute prediction, and charisma without epistemic integrity.

## Extractor
Represents scam systems, predatory education, exploitative platforms, and extraction from inexperienced participants.

These are antagonist categories, not yet locked permanent characters.

# EC.36 — CAST POWER ASYMMETRY

No universal power scale.

Different characters dominate different dimensions:
- GLX — judgment, adaptability, cross-layer interpretation
- CEREBUS — scale, memory, structure
- Risk Warden — survival logic
- Macro Cartographer — long-horizon context
- Rival — alternative edge
- Bull/Bear — directional force
- Fear — threat amplification

This creates believable conflict.

# EC.38 — CHARACTER INTRODUCTION RULE

Do not introduce a named character until they provide at least one necessary perspective, recurring conflict, audience gateway, emotional stake, specialist domain, or institutional personalization.

Otherwise use world populations.

---

# EC.39 — CAST SIZE DISCIPLINE

Early GLX WORLD should feel:

> **deep, not crowded**

A small cast with clear functions is stronger than twenty names.

---

# EC.40 — RECOGNITION RULE

Every Tier A/Tier B recurring character eventually needs:
- silhouette,
- color logic,
- posture,
- voice,
- recurring prop/motif,
- emotional rhythm.

Five-second recognition applies beyond GLX too.
