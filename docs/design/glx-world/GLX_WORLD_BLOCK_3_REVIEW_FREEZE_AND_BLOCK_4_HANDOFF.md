# 2 — CORE ENTITY PHILOSOPHY

> **Characters make the world emotionally legible. Ontology keeps the characters intellectually honest.**

Every recurring entity must represent something meaningful, have limitations, possess a native layer, have a defined relationship to GLX, behave consistently with ontology, and remain compatible with financial/psychological logic.

# 4 — BULL / BEAR FREEZE

Bull and Bear are hard-frozen as directional archetypes rather than moral alignments.

Bull = sustained upward directional pressure.  
Bear = sustained downward directional pressure.

They cannot permanently die, do not control the Market, can weaken/fail/exhaust, can manifest regionally, and may appear as Herds or Prime manifestations.

GLX's relationship evolves:

Fear → Fight → Study → Alignment → Dual Competence → Respect.

> **You do not beat the Bull. You do not beat the Bear. You learn when each is moving, when neither has control, and whether the risk of joining them is worth taking.**

# 7 — INSTITUTIONAL FREEZE

The institutional world is an ecosystem, not a cabal.

> **Size creates influence. Information creates advantage. Constraints create behavior. Incentives create conflict. None of them create omnipotence.**

Institutional actors must be written through objectives, capital, horizon, constraints, risk, information, liquidity need, and adaptability.

No singular Smart Money villain.

Villainy comes from choices: fraud, intentional deception, exploitation, hidden risk, corruption, or reckless abuse of power.

# 11 — KNOWLEDGE ASYMMETRY

Characters must not share one brain.

Every named character needs strengths, blind spots, and information-access limits.

Production should distinguish:

## Canon Truth
What is internally established.

## Character Truth
What GLX/CEREBUS currently believe.

## Audience Truth
What has actually been revealed.

This three-truth model is essential for mystery management.

---

# 12 — CONTINUITY LAW

Characters remember. Relationships evolve. Damage persists. Knowledge accumulates.

A future continuity ledger should track relationship state, damage/injury, knowledge learned, unresolved conflict, promises, role, last major appearance, and visual-state changes.

Scalable storytelling cannot reset characters every episode.
