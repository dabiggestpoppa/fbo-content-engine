# GLX WORLD — MARKET WORLD SUB-BIBLE v0.1

## Status
Foundational sub-bible for Block 2.3.

## Purpose
The Market World is the primary mass-audience fantasy layer of GLX WORLD and the visual translation layer for CEREBUS ontology.

It must function simultaneously as:
- fantasy setting,
- intuitive market model,
- educational system,
- living knowledge graph,
- long-term story engine.

> **A fantasy fan should be able to love the world without understanding finance, while a market expert should recognize a coherent model underneath it.**

---

# MW.1 — WORLD IDENTITY

The Market World is:

> **A living financial civilization built on shifting geography, moving capital, changing weather, unstable relationships, and incomplete knowledge.**

Stable landmarks exist. Stable conditions do not.

---

# MW.2 — FOUR-DEPTH STANDARD

Every major realm, landmark, ritual, hazard, or creature should work at four depths:

1. Story — can a non-trader understand the stakes?
2. Intuition — does the metaphor teach naturally?
3. Mechanics — does it correspond to real behavior?
4. Ontology — can CEREBUS model it as states, nodes, edges, probabilities, constraints, or transitions?

# MW.4 — SESSION CIVILIZATIONS

Sessions are temporal civilizations. Their architecture remains while activity changes with the Market Clock.

A realm can be asleep, awakening, active, overlapping, or closing.

Time changes access, population, and opportunity — not just sky color.

# MW.23 — MARKET ATLAS

- stable landmarks,
- temporary routes,
- regime overlays,
- uncertain territory,
- historical scars,

- dotted = probable,
- fading = uncertain,

- black = unknown.
